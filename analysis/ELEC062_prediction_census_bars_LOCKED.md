# ELEC-062 — The distinctive-prediction census: locked criteria

## Commission
NUCQ-001 recorded months ago that the corpus possesses no distinctive testable
prediction. ELEC-061 retired the one claim that had contested this. The
statement has never been checked systematically against the whole registry.

## Locked criteria (a claim QUALIFIES only if all four hold)
C1 QUANTITATIVE: it states a number or a definite sign/shape, not a direction
   of effect or a consistency demonstration.
C2 DISTINCTIVE: its OBSERVABLE OUTCOME differs from what standard physics (or
   MOND, where the domain overlaps) predicts. A claim that DERIVES a number
   others fit does not qualify on that ground alone -- deriving g_dagger and
   measuring g_dagger are different achievements, and only the second
   discriminates. This is the criterion most likely to be softened and must
   not be.
C3 CHECKABLE IN PRINCIPLE: an existing or near-term instrument could return
   the discriminating measurement.
C4 LIVE: not already refuted, retired, or withdrawn.

## Tiers to report
T1 QUALIFIES on all four.
T2 QUALIFIES but for C3 (distinctive, live, not currently measurable).
T3 DERIVATION-DISTINCTIVE only: derives what others fit, same observable.
T4 CONSTRAINT, not prediction: bounds a parameter, predicts no value.
T5 REFUTED/RETIRED: kept for the record.

## Locked bars
B1 Report the T1 list explicitly and completely. If it is empty, say so.
B2 No claim may be promoted a tier on the grounds that it is the corpus's
   best available. The census reports what is there.
B3 Report the count in each tier and name the sectors that contribute nothing.
B4 State whether NUCQ-001's standing assessment is CONFIRMED or OVERTURNED.
