# GRV-065 — GRV-064 downgraded from closure to candidate obstruction. Locked bars

## Why this claim exists
GRV-064 declared the twist route "genuinely closed". An external reviewer
identified three steps treated as derived that are not, and two escape closures
that are not decisive. The criticism is correct and this claim executes the
downgrade. This is the second over-conclusion in the same arc (GRV-059 was the
first), and the second caught by a reader rather than by me.

## The three unestablished steps
U1 THE CHARACTERISTIC LENGTH IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY THE STRAND SPACING.
   ell^2 ~ (couple-stress modulus)/(rotation-locking modulus) is a RATIO OF
   MODULI. It is often ASSOCIATED with microstructure size, not identical to it.
   If the locking modulus is zero, small, symmetry-protected or scale-dependent,
   ell can be macroscopic or divergent. GRV-064 ASSERTED ell ~ w; it must be
   DERIVED as ell = C a with C fixed and modest.
U2 CALUGAREANU CONSERVATION IS NOT LOCAL LOCKING. Lk = Tw + Wr is a GLOBAL
   TOPOLOGICAL LEDGER. It does not determine the local energy penalty for
   phi - (1/2) curl u, which is what sets the locking modulus. An exact
   conserved integer coexists with soft twist-writhe redistribution, weak local
   frame-backbone coupling, gapless twist waves and large characteristic
   lengths. GRV-064's "tightest tie available" conflated topological bookkeeping
   with energetic stiffness.
U3 SCREENING THE RELATIVE ROTATION DOES NOT SCREEN EVERY ROTATIONAL FIELD.
   Micropolar systems have coupled displacement and microrotation sectors; the
   nonclassical part may decay exponentially while the ORDINARY ELASTIC
   DISPLACEMENT retains an algebraic far field. If u_phi ~ sin(theta)/r^2 is an
   ordinary displacement solution, its curl keeps a 1/r^3 tail regardless of
   what the Cosserat correction does.

## The two escape closures that are not decisive
E1' NEUTRALITY does not eliminate a gravitomagnetic DIPOLE. A globally neutral
    medium supports local positive and negative twist, dipole moments and
    circulating currents. Zero net charge does not forbid long-range multipoles.
E2' "LINKING IS SCALAR, FRAME DRAGGING NEEDS A VECTOR" IS NOT A NO-GO. The
    rotating source already supplies the axial vector J, so a structure like
    Lk J or a constitutive pseudoscalar times J is available. Scalar-versus-
    vector character alone proves nothing.
