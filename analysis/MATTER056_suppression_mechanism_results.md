## Step 1 — the derivation (target not invoked)

The ambient weave carries zero-point energy in its transverse modes to the
mesh cutoff. Inserting a strand displaces the medium over its own
cross-section; for a dilute perturbation the fractional energy shift is the
fraction of mode-carrying medium displaced:

    displaced cross-section = πr²
    available per cell      = a²
    λ_derived = π(r/a)² = 2.776e-6

Parameter-free, registered geometry only, power 2 in the thinness (inside the
MATTER042 high-power rule). **Note the absence:** no factor of α appears
anywhere. The coupling enters this corpus through the quantum-*area*
relation, not through a geometric displacement.
