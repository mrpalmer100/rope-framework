# ELEC-059 — Which length sets the causal reach, and does rope width close the
# ceiling near-miss? Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-058 left one order-unity gap in a sector where every other gap is 1e4:
at the Lorentz ceiling the recruitment shortfall is 3.46. A wound rope is
roughly twice a single strand in width, so if the causal reach is set by ROPE
diameter rather than STRAND spacing the gap might close. A first estimate gave
1.73 on the naive reading and 2.446 on a self-consistent one -- close enough
that the definition of the coherent unit now matters, which it never did at 1e4.

## The question, stated precisely
ELEC-047 derived tau0 ~ 1.95 in units of w/c on a barrier whose coordinate is
the SEPARATION between two strands. Is that w a strand spacing (in which case
the rope-width escape requires the MEDIUM to be made of ropes, a different
hypothesis) or an incidental unit? And if the medium's coherent unit is a
k-strand rope of diameter D, what is the shortfall as a function of k and D?

## Locked bars
B1 (PROVENANCE OF tau0). Read the derivation, not the number: identify what
   physical length the barrier coordinate measures. Report whether tau0 is
   tied to strand spacing by construction.
B2 (THE GENERAL UNIT MODEL, derived before evaluating). Let the medium's
   coherent unit be a rope of k strands, diameter D = f w, carrying tension
   k T0. Derive the shortfall ratio R_c/(c tau0) as a function of k and f.
   REPORT ANY CANCELLATION EXPLICITLY -- if D drops out, the entire premise of
   this session (that width helps) is wrong and must be said plainly.
B3 (THE THRESHOLD). Solve for the k that closes the ceiling case, and compare
   it to the corpus's own electron constituent count (ELEC-041: TWO strands).
B4 (SCOPE). State whether any of this bears on the electron sector's 5.2-decade
   gap (ELEC-057) or on small a (ELEC-058). If it does not, say so: this
   concerns only the ceiling, where ELEC-057 already excludes the hbar sector
   by 6.7x on the Lorentz bound alone.
B5 (HONESTY). A medium of k-strand ropes is a DIFFERENT MEDIUM HYPOTHESIS from
   ELEC-038's declaration, not a refinement of it. Any k that closes the gap
   must be labelled as the new postulate it is.
