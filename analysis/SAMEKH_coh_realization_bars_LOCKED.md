      R1 star-with-bare-cost: the misplaced nucleon keeps NUC-021's
         diagonal cost 4 eps and couples off-diagonally to N degenerate
         misplacement configurations. Staggering = 4 eps minus the
         coherent gain.
      R2 rank-1 condensate blocking: N pair states coupled all-to-all;
         the odd nucleon blocks one. Staggering = E_gs(N-1) - E_gs(N)
         relative shift.
      R3 degenerate seniority: E(n, s) = -(G/4)(n - s)(2 Omega - n - s + 2);
         staggering from the seniority cost of the odd nucleon.
      R4 pure off-diagonal: in the PAIRING channel the odd nucleon's
         diagonal cost is zero and its energy is entirely the
         hybridization magnitude with N mediator-coupled configurations.
         (The diagonal cross-sublattice cost is NOT deleted from the
         corpus: under R4 it lives in the ASYMMETRY channel, where D2's
         surface dilution applies. R4 is a channel SEPARATION, not a
         cancellation.)
