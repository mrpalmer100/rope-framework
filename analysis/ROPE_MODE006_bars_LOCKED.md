# ROPE-MODE-006 locked bars — local topology-surgery fingerprint

The first four bound states are compared for a certified Hopf-linked rope and a minimally displaced unlinked control produced by a local crossing surgery. The central potential and tubular coupling are unchanged from ROPE-MODE-004.

Locked parameters:

- central coupling `alpha = 12.0`
- central softening `epsilon = 0.30`
- rope coupling `beta = 0.50`
- tube width `sigma = 0.16`
- fixed spatial spacing `h = 0.25`
- box half-widths `4.0` and `5.0`
- first 4 eigenmodes

Success requires all bars:

1. Linked and surgery controls have certified linking numbers near `-1` and `0`, respectively, with minimum separation above `0.060`.
2. The surgery control is geometrically matched: curve RMS displacement below `0.05`, total-length change below `1%`, radial second-moment change below `2%`, and normalized quadrupole change below `8%`.
3. Outer-shell probability is below `0.1%` for every reported mode and control on the larger box.
4. Excitation gaps change by less than `1%` from box 4 to box 5.
5. Linked-minus-surgery gap contrasts exceed three times combined domain uncertainty for at least two of the three excited modes.
6. Any significant contrasts preserve sign and remain within 50% in magnitude under domain enlargement.

A positive result supports a topology-specific fingerprint under a minimally changed geometry. A negative result means the earlier translated-control signal cannot yet be attributed to topology.
