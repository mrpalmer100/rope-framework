# COMMISSION PE -- THE LABEL-BLINDNESS AUDIT: BARS (LOCKED BEFORE SWEEPING)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before any claim is classified. FND-054's outstanding
obligation: GRANT-N2 added an N-fold label degree of freedom at the
ontology's base, and every existing derivation must be checked for whether
that addition disturbs it. FND-053 priced the audit as expected-cheap by
the FND-050 argument (the derivations never mention labels). THIS BAR
REFUSES THAT EXPECTATION AS A FINDING: "expected cheap" is a prior, not a
result, and the audit is run to test it.*

## The audit's actual hypothesis (stated so it can fail)

The naive argument -- "no derivation mentions labels, so none is
disturbed" -- IS INSUFFICIENT, and the bar says so before the sweep:
a derivation need not mention a degree of freedom to depend on HOW MANY
there are. The corpus contains kills and calibrations that turn on
COUNTING (state counts, mode counts, strand counts, dof sums). An N-fold
internal multiplicity multiplies exactly those. The audit's real job is
to find them.

## Risk classes (fixed before the sweep; a claim may fall in several)

- **R-STATE:** the claim's conclusion turns on a COUNT OF STATES or
  POLARIZATIONS (kill-by-state-count, dof matching). HIGHEST RISK: an
  N-fold multiplicity changes the count directly.
- **R-MODE:** the claim sums or integrates over MODES (zero-point
  energies, mode-filling, spectral sums). An N-fold multiplicity
  multiplies the sum.
- **R-COUNT:** the claim uses a STRAND or ROPE COUNT as a calibrated or
  derived number (n, N_ropes, coverage thresholds, Sigma normalizations).
- **R-STAT:** the claim uses entropy, statistics, or multiplicity.
- **R-BLIND:** the claim's derivation uses only geometry, topology,
  tension, or continuum mechanics with no counting of internal states.

## Method (pre-committed)

1. Mechanical keyword sweep of claims.yaml for counting language, with
   the matched terms reported. The sweep is a NET, not the verdict.
2. Every claim the sweep flags is adjudicated by reading it, and the
   adjudication -- DISTURBED / SAFE-BY-ARGUMENT / OPEN -- is recorded
   with the argument named.
3. A claim is SAFE-BY-ARGUMENT only if a stated reason survives: either
   the label is a passive internal index that cannot be excited at the
   energies in question (a DECOUPLING argument, which must be named as an
   assumption if it is not registered), or the counted object is not
   multiplied by labels.

## Bars

- The audit PASSES CHEAP only if zero claims land DISTURBED.
- Any DISTURBED claim must be registered with its correction pointer, and
  the grant's price is re-stated to the author at the new figure.
- If the audit finds a class of risk that cannot be settled without a
  further decision, it must NAME that decision and leave it (standing
  rule), not resolve it.
- The audit may not adopt, widen, or narrow GRANT-N2.
