## THE QUESTION

The spectator test established the weave (V2). The weave is made of
ropes; an atom participates by local connections. The commission asks:
at what coordination number z does an atom stop being merely
CONNECTED (reachable by signals) and become EMBEDDED (subject to and
sourcing full weave physics)? Sub-questions, each with pre-registered
tests:

## PRE-REGISTERED ANALYTIC POSITIONS (stated before computation)

- z = 1 (pendant atom): CONNECTED, NOT EMBEDDED. Its physics is 1D
  waveguide physics: one interaction channel, endpoint impedance
  sqrt(T0 mu) instead of the medium's response, and it does not
  source a 1/r^2 field from its own location -- its disturbance
  enters the weave at the anchor and radiates from THERE. An antenna
  on a cable, not a point in space.
- z = 2..3: chains and marginal structures; effectively
  low-dimensional; no isotropic environment.
- z >= 4 WITH PRETENSION: candidate minimal weave. Maxwell counting
  says central-force rigidity in 3D needs z = 6, but PRETENSION
  stabilizes sub-isostatic networks (the spider-web fact); the
  framework's weave is tensioned by construction.
- z = 6: the tension-independent guarantee, and the effective
  coordination of the corpus's own cubic-stencil operators.

## THE LOCKED TESTS

C1 RIGIDITY PERCOLATION UNDER PRETENSION: on random networks of the
   registered strand mechanics (T0, mu, finite contact), measure the
   z at which a transverse disturbance propagates with a finite speed
   (medium behavior) vs diffuses/localizes (floppy behavior), as a
   function of pretension. BAR: the transition z, with pretension
   dependence, measured on at least three network sizes with the
   size-drift displayed.
C2 SOURCE FIDELITY: an oscillating atom of coordination z; measure
   the far-field amplitude decay exponent p(z) (1/r^p). BAR:
   EMBEDDED requires p = 1 within 5 percent (3D spreading); pendant
   prediction p = 0 along its rope.
C3 STATIC FIDELITY: the static conditioning of a z-coordinated
   source; measure the force-law exponent on a distant test knot.
   BAR: EMBEDDED requires 1/r^2 within 5 percent over a decade.
C4 IMPEDANCE: the drag coefficient on transverse motion of the
   z-atom vs the medium prediction. BAR: within 10 percent of the
   continuum value at the declared embedding z.
C5 ISOTROPY EMERGENCE: angular variance of C2/C3 responses vs z and
   vs distance. BAR: report the (z, r) domain where anisotropy
   < 10 percent; no pass/fail, this is a map.
C6 DEFECT PHYSICS: one under-coordinated atom inside an embedded
   weave (a z = 1 pendant hanging off a z >= 6 bulk). Measure how
   its effective inertia, coupling, and sourcing differ from bulk
   atoms. PRE-REGISTERED INTERPRETATION RULE: differences are
   reported as measured; NO identification with any particle-physics
