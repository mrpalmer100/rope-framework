# ELEC-070 — Derrick in 3+1: does anything survive? Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-069 named the honest next step and warned it was not small: Derrick's
theorem is dimension-dependent and the whole ELEC-068/069 arc is 1+1. This
redoes it in three dimensions.

## The scaling, fixed before computing
For psi(x) -> psi(x/L) in D dimensions with d^D x -> L^D:
  gradient term      |grad psi|^2  ->  L^(D-2)
  quartic            |grad psi|^4  ->  L^(D-4)
  sextic             |grad psi|^6  ->  L^(D-6)
  internal frequency omega^2 psi^2 ->  L^D
D = 1 reproduces ELEC-068's A/L - B/L^3 + C L. D = 3 gives A L - B/L + C L^3.

## Locked bars
B1 THE 3D VERDICT ON ELEC-068's MECHANISM. In D = 3, dE/dL = A + B/L^2 + 3C L^2
   with every term POSITIVE, so E is monotone and the infimum is at L -> 0 where
   -B/L diverges. If that is what the algebra says, then THE DYNAMICAL
   STABILIZATION OF ELEC-068 IS A ONE-DIMENSIONAL ARTIFACT and must be reported
   as such, retracting nothing that was true in 1+1 but withdrawing its
   relevance to a real particle.
B2 THE SEXTIC'S SIGN, computed not assumed: expand the strain energy to sixth
   order in psi' after eliminating the longitudinal field and read the
   coefficient. A POSITIVE sextic scales as +L^(D-6) = +L^-3 in 3D and diverges
   at small L, which is the Skyrme-class mechanism. Report the sign and the
   condition on k and T0 under which it holds.
B3 THE 3D TEST WITH THE SEXTIC: does E = A L - B/L + D/L^3 admit a minimum?
   Report the stationary point and its stability.
B4 THE CAVEAT THAT MUST NOT BE OMITTED: the EXACT elimination of the
   longitudinal field is degenerate -- solving dE/du' = 0 exactly gives
   eps = -T0/k and a CONSTANT energy independent of psi', because an
   inextensible medium can absorb any transverse displacement by shortening.
   The perturbative elimination used here is therefore an approximation whose
   validity is not established, and the sextic coefficient inherits that
   uncertainty. State it plainly.
B5 No claim about the electron. Report only what the scaling says.
