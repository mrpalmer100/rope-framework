## The universality test (the finding)

A substrate-intrinsic cutting time is a property of the **medium**. The same
weave underlies every apparatus, so such a time must be universal — identical
across platforms and untunable by engineering.

Observed coherence times span roughly **10 orders** across platforms
(ensembles, trapped ions, rare-earth crystals, NV centers), and the spread is
systematic rather than noisy: it tracks isolation, temperature, and material
engineering. It tracks the **environment**.

**Verdict: the shape of the prediction is excluded before any number is
computed.** Coherence is demonstrably an engineerable quantity, and a
property of the medium is not engineerable.

## Secondary note (magnitude — reported, not relied upon)

The corpus's only registered time built from the mesh is a/c = 2.00e-25 s.
That is ~19 orders below even the shortest bracket and ~29 below the longest.
The magnitude fails too, and fails by an amount that **varies with platform** —
which is the universality verdict restated in numbers, not an independent
second failure.

## The pre-banned rescue, named and refused

One could posit an unfixed suppression carrying the 19–29 orders. Refused by
bar 4 and independently by today's results: FND-MATTER-042 found the corpus's
suppression candidates unfixed and FND-MATTER-047 exhausted its channels, so
borrowing one here is circular. And a suppression that itself had to vary by
platform would not be a substrate property at all — the universality objection
returning as arithmetic.

**The timescale lever is dead**, and more decisively, it *should* be:
decoherence is environmental in this picture as in the standard one, so the
ribbon is cut by the environment on the environment's schedule, not by the
medium on its own.

1. The ontology is consistent with platform-dependent coherence precisely
   *because* it predicts no intrinsic time — **consistency by silence**, worth
   stating but never citable as evidence for the picture.
2. **QB-034's conclusion is now closed from both sides:** no lever in the
   decay shape (degenerate or killed), none in the timescale (wrong shape of
   prediction entirely). The repeater-network question is settled negatively
   and completely, with benchmarks on both halves.
3. **The session's positive:** "cutting the ribbon" must be an environmental
   coupling event, so any future ribbon dynamics owes a coupling term to the
   ambient weave — the same destination FND-MATTER-053 sent ΔE_zp tonight.
   Two independent sessions now point the same way: **the action is in the
   ambient medium, not the object's interior.**
