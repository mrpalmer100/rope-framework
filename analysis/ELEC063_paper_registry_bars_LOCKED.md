# ELEC-063 — Reconciling the predictions paper with the registry: locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-062's census reported three distinctive predictions corpus-wide. It was
WRONG, and the error is methodological: it walked claims.yaml only and never
consulted papers/falsifiable_predictions.docx, which states seventeen. This
session reconciles the two and reports the divergence in both directions.

## Locked bars
B1 (THE CENSUS CORRECTED). Apply ELEC-062's four criteria -- unchanged, not
   relaxed -- to the paper's seventeen entries. Report the corrected T1 list.
B2 (THE REGISTRY GAP, in the paper's favour). Identify paper predictions with
   NO corresponding registry claim. The paper asserts that every statement is
   "traceable to the machine-readable registry (claims.yaml), the corpus's
   single source of truth". If that sentence is false for any headline
   prediction, say so plainly: a traceability claim that does not hold is a
   defect of the same kind this corpus spent tonight fixing elsewhere.
B3 (THE PAPER GAP, in the registry's favour). Identify registry T1 predictions
   ABSENT from the paper.
B4 (NO SOFTENING). ELEC-062's distinctiveness criterion stands: deriving a
   number others fit does not qualify. Paper entries labelled structural,
   scale-open, provisional, or consistency-tier are classified on their
   content, not their placement.
B5 (WHAT IS OWED). State the corrective action for each divergence, and record
   the census's own failure mode as the lesson.
