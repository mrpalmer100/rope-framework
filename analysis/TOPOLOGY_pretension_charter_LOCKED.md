## THE QUESTION (the axis the second brick honestly deferred)

In the scalar sector, membership's dynamic threshold measured
z_c = 3.75 +/- 0.25 and pretension could not matter (it only
rescales c). In the VECTOR sector -- displacements as 3-vectors,
bonds as springs -- Maxwell counting says central-force rigidity
needs z = 2d = 6, but PRETENSION stabilizes sub-isostatic networks
(a spider web is floppy by counting yet carries transverse waves
because it is taut). The framework's weave is tensioned by
construction. So: is the vector membership threshold z_c a
function of pretension?

## SHARED PARAMETERS (no-rescue inherited)

Linearized bond stiffness about the pretensioned state:
  K_bond = k * (b b^T) + (T0 / L) * (I - b b^T)
(k: spring constant along the bond direction b; T0/L: the tension
term acting on transverse displacements). Shared k = m = a = 1;
the single control is tau = T0 / (k a), identical for every bond;
NOTHING may depend on z or on results.

## PRE-REGISTERED POSITIONS (stated before computation)

- tau -> 0: the vector transverse channel loses its restoring
  force off-axis; floppy modes proliferate below the Maxwell
  point; expect z_c to RISE toward ~6 (central-force rigidity).
- tau ~ 1: the tension term is as strong as the spring term; the
  transverse sector approaches the scalar problem (the tension
  projector IS a scalar Laplacian weight); expect z_c to FALL
  toward the scalar 3.75 +/- 0.25.
- Therefore the committed expectation is W1 (z_c decreasing in
  tau). W2 (flat z_c) would falsify the spider-web mechanism
  inside this framework's own mechanics and would be the more
  surprising, more valuable outcome.
