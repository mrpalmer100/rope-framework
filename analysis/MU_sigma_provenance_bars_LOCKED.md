*Locked 2026-08-10, before any computation. Charter approved by Mark.*

## Commission
Sigma is the corpus's one remaining free scale (FND-017), with two registered
candidates 28% apart: the lattice-anchored 3.61–3.70e35 J/m³ (ELEC-052/081) and
the framework's Sigma-route 5.10e35 J/m³ (QGATE-005 Arm 2 / QGATE-007's
inverted prediction). QGATE-018 ruled that no vacuum experiment in reach decides
the value and that the route is a better CALCULATION. This commission asks
whether the first better calculation is an AUDIT: the Sigma-route candidate was
forced by the trio {tension additivity; Lorentz bound a <= 1e-16 m; n_t = 111},
and n_t = 111 has since been killed in the reconnection chain (ELEC-045/047)
and re-pinned from the measured flux-tube width at n = 115–236 (NUCQ-003).
If every path to 5.1e35 runs through a dead number, the 28% is not a tension —
it is a stale registration.

## Locked bars
B1 THE PROVENANCE GRAPH. Reconstruct the full derivation DAG of Sigma = 5.1e35:
   every input claim, with its current status (live / superseded / Failed).
   State explicitly whether n_t = 111 is load-bearing, or whether the candidate
   has an independent leg (the QGATE-008 four-input surface, the hbar-serving
   funnel, or any other registered route).

B2 THE KILL-INHERITANCE RULE, stated in advance. If every path to 5.1e35 runs
   through a claim now Failed or superseded, the candidate is DEMOTED to
   historical and the verdict must say so. No rescue by fresh re-derivation
   inside this session: a new derivation of a large Sigma would be a NEW
   candidate for a NEW commission, not a defense of this one.

B3 THE FORWARD RE-DERIVATION. Run Arm 2's own arithmetic
   (Sigma = 3 T_tube / (n a^2) with T_tube from the registered chain, at the
   Lorentz bound) with the SURVIVING strand counts: NUCQ-003's width-pinned
   band n = 115–236 and the lattice-data values n = 152–156. Report the Sigma
   band the surviving constraints actually force.

B4 THE CONFRONTATION, outcomes pre-committed and reported whichever falls:
   (1) The surviving-input band overlaps the lattice-anchored 3.61–3.70e35:
       the two candidates were ONE number seen through a dead input. Sigma is
       pinned to the lattice band; FND-017's "one open number" becomes "one
       measured number, conversion-model dependence stated"; the parameter
       card's branch structure is retired to historical.
   (2) The bands exclude each other on surviving inputs alone: the tension is
       real and deeper than n_t. Register it and name the next arbiter.
   (3) The audit finds a genuinely independent leg for 5.1e35: the fork
       stands. Report why the "better calculation" must go elsewhere (an
       independent lattice determination on different data).
   (4) The audit itself fails to close (provenance untraceable, arithmetic
       ambiguous): register Failed-and-kept with the obstruction named.

B5 HONESTY. Pinning Sigma inside this session cannot silently promote the
   lattice band's own known dependencies. They must appear on the claim's
   face: (a) the strand-count-follows-energy-density identification at the
   scale-chain fork (ELEC-053's registered load-bearing assumption); (b) the
   sech^2 conversion model and its tested 2.2% profile spread (ELEC-081 B3);
   (c) same-data/same-distance caveat — ELEC-081 tested the estimator, not
   the measurement; an independent lattice determination remains the named
   external check. Additionally: if n_t = 111's death is found to be
   scoped to the reconnection-hbar cell rather than to the strand count as
   such, that scoping must be stated and the verdict adjusted accordingly —
   a kill's scope is part of its content.
