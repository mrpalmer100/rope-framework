# GRV-063 — The falloff audit: locked bars

## Commission
GRV-062 named the quotient computation. Attempting it required fixing the
multipole orders first, and doing so exposed an error in GRV-060's scoring.

## Locked bars
B1 FALLOFF, stated carefully. |(J x r)/r^3| = J sin(theta)/r^2 -- the r^3 is in
   the FORMULA, the FALLOFF is 1/r^2. Determine what GRV-060 actually matched.
B2 THE ELASTIC LADDER: rotlet (torque monopole) and torque dipole, with their
   displacement and rotation falloffs.
B3 THE LEMMA'S BITE: GRV-017/GRV-020 establish that a static isolated defect
   exerts zero net force AND zero net torque. Determine whether that kills the
   rotlet, and what the ladder then starts at.
B4 THE ESCAPE, and this bar exists because GRV-059 concluded without checking
   one: is TORQUE even the right source? In GR frame dragging is sourced by
   angular MOMENTUM, not by torque, and a body of constant J exerts no torque.
   Determine whether standard elasticity has a source of that kind, and whether
   the corpus's medium has structure standard elasticity lacks.
B5 VERDICT: correct GRV-060's scoring, and state the remaining question without
   deciding it in either direction.
