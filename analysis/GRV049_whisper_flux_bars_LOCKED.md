# GRV-049 — Committing the whisper's flux: locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-064 left GRV-040 as one of two surviving T1 predictions and named its
uncommitted flux as the corpus's highest-value open move -- the only promotion
available through the corpus's own work. GRV-040's S3 already states the law,
L = f (dM/dt) c^2 T_grey with f <= 1 by energetics. This commits the numbers
and asks whether anything could see it.

## Locked bars
B1 THE FREQUENCY, per source. omega_inf = 0.23 kappa with kappa = c^3/(4GM).
   Report nu for a stellar-mass hole, a GW-scale binary remnant, Sgr A* and
   M87*. State which detector bands these fall in.
B2 THE LUMINOSITY, per source, at Eddington accretion with the standard
   radiative efficiency, using GRV-040's own greybody T_grey = 0.04-0.11.
B3 THE CONFRONTATION, and it must be run in whichever direction the numbers
   point: compare L_whisper to the observed accretion budget. IF the predicted
   luminosity is a large fraction of the budget, then OBSERVATION ALREADY
   BOUNDS f, and the prediction converts from a signal into a bound. Report
   that bound.
B4 THE CHANNEL QUESTION, which decides the tier. Does the whisper couple to
   any detector? Two branches, both to be priced:
   (A) it couples electromagnetically -- then B3's bound applies and is severe;
   (B) it is a medium excitation that does not couple to EM -- then it is
   unbounded but also UNDETECTABLE, which fails ELEC-062's checkability
   criterion and costs GRV-040 its T1 status.
   NO THIRD BRANCH may be invented to preserve the tier.
B5 THE VERDICT on GRV-040's tier, stated whichever way it falls, including if
   it means the corpus is left with ONE prediction.
