# ELEC-076 — Does the surviving soliton need the fast channel? Locked bars

## Commission
A direct question about the line ELEC-066..075: the soliton that survived as a
structural result (a stable localized finite-energy solution in this medium) --
does it require the longitudinal/superluminal channel that ELEC-067 established
matter can couple to, or does it stand without it?

## What must be checked, fixed before computing
Two energy functionals have been used in this line and they were never compared:
  (i)  ELEC-068 B1: eliminate the longitudinal field via the cubic vertex,
       giving quartic coefficient -(k - T0)^2/(8k);
  (ii) ELEC-071/074: the lab-parametrization tension energy T0(sqrt(1+p^2) - 1),
       whose quartic is -T0/8 and which contains NO k at all.
If these disagree in the regime the corpus occupies, then the exact results of
ELEC-073/074/075 rest on (ii) and the fast-channel framing of ELEC-067..070
was not what produced them. That must be reported as an internal inconsistency
of the line, not glossed.
