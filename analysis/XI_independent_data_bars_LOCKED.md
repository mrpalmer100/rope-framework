# Commission XI — the independent-data confrontation: locked bars

*Locked 2026-08-10, before any literature search or data retrieval. This is
the one external check FND-030/031 left standing behind the Sigma pinning.*

## Commission
ELEC-052 measured R_eq = 0.407(14) fm and ELEC-081 confirmed the ESTIMATOR to
1.3% — but both used the same data (arXiv 2409.20168, Baker et al.) at the
same distance. The pinning of Sigma therefore rests on ONE measurement. This
session hunts an INDEPENDENT determination: different collaboration or
different ensembles, ideally full QCD at or near physical masses, and
confronts the pinned band. The search happens AFTER these bars are locked, so
the confrontation rule cannot be tuned to what the literature turns out to say.

## Independence criteria, fixed in advance
I1 A determination counts as INDEPENDENT only if it is not the 2409.20168
   dataset and not a reanalysis of it. Same-group earlier papers on different
   ensembles count as PARTIALLY independent (data independent, method shared)
   and must be labeled as such.
I2 Gauge-theory tier, fixed: (a) full QCD 2+1(+1) flavors = tier 1;
   (b) SU(3) pure gauge = tier 2 (usable, quenching caveat on the face);
   (c) other groups/dimensions (SU(2), 3d) = tier 3, context only, no verdict
   weight.
I3 The quantity: the transverse chromoelectric-energy profile width at
   mid-plane, converted to the mass-density equivalent radius R_eq by the SAME
   registered conversion ELEC-051/052 used (E^2-weighted uniform-cylinder;
   sech^2 profile where a fit is needed). No new conversion invented mid-session.

## Locked bars
B1 THE SEARCH is reported as performed: what was searched, what was found,
   what was excluded and why (by I1/I2). An empty result is a result: if no
   tier-1 or tier-2 independent determination exists in the literature, the
   session registers that the external check CANNOT currently be run, and the
   pinning's single-measurement status stays on the face — no downgrade of
   the bar to tier 3 to manufacture a verdict.
B2 THE CONVERSION: published widths arrive in mixed conventions (Gaussian
   sqrt(w^2), sech^2 scale, RMS, Clem lambda). Each is converted to R_eq with
   the conversion factor stated per source. If a source's convention cannot be
   unambiguously mapped, it is reported and EXCLUDED, not guessed.
B3 THE CONFRONTATION BAR, pre-committed: the reference is R_eq = 0.402-0.407 fm.
   - CONFIRMED: independent R_eq within +/-10% (the registered conversion
     model-dependence class) -> the external check passes; Sigma's pinning
     gains its first independent leg; the same-data caveat is discharged to
     the degree of the source's independence tier.
   - TENSION: 10-25% deviation -> registered as a live tension with the
     deviation propagated to Sigma as R_eq^-2; the pinning stands but carries
     the tension on its face.
   - CONTRADICTED: > 25% deviation from a tier-1 source -> the pinned band is
     WOUNDED; Sigma reverts to a band spanning both determinations and FND-030's
     "one measured number" language is retracted to "one number, two
     measurements in conflict." Reported at full prominence if it falls.
B4 DISTANCE MATCHING: ELEC-052's verdict-bearing distance is d = 0.7 fm
   (quark-antiquark separation), where the width is on the intrinsic plateau.
   Independent widths at substantially different separations are corrected
   only if the source itself provides the d-dependence; otherwise the mismatch
   is stated as a systematic, not silently absorbed.
B5 HONESTY: whatever the verdict, the session states plainly which tier
   supplied it and what remains unchecked. A tier-2 pass does not discharge
   the quenching question; a partially-independent pass does not discharge the
   method-sharing question. The claim face must let a reader reconstruct
   exactly how independent the check was.
