**The reviewer reconstructed a Derived result unaided.** That is worth

## Z2 -- IS r = 0 REGULARIZED? YES, and not in any of the ways the reviewer listed.

The reviewer's list of possibilities (helix unwinding, radius
collapsing, neighbours rearranging, order parameter vanishing) does not

**ELEC-074 (Modeled): there is no r = 0 to regularize. The exact object

**The field exists only outside r0 = sqrt(C).** The profile
p(x) = x^-2/sqrt(1 - x^-4) diverges at the CORE BOUNDARY, not at the
origin.

ELEC-073's earlier cusp at the origin was an artifact of sixth-order
truncation and is withdrawn by ELEC-074 -- so the corpus already caught
and corrected the very singularity the reviewer worried about.

The winding does not terminate at a point. **It terminates on a sphere
of finite radius**, and the exterior solution simply does not extend
inside it. That is a genuine regularization, and it is the corpus's
registered position.
