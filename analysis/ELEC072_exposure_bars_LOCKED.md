# ELEC-072 — Reducing the exposure: does existence need the attractive sign? Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-071 found the quartic's sign parametrization-dependent and left the whole
soliton line resting on FND-REL-002, recommending nothing further until the
exposure was accepted or reduced. This attempts the reduction, and by the
cheapest available route: asking whether the 3D soliton needs the attraction at
all, before reaching for the topological argument that was the named fallback.

## The observation to be tested
ELEC-070 established two facts with different standings: the quartic is
CONTINGENT on parametrization (-1/8 lab, +1/8 arclength) while the sextic is
ROBUST (+1/16 in both). In 3D the terms scale as +A L (gradient), sB/L (quartic,
sign s), +D/L^3 (sextic). The sextic diverges at small L and the gradient at
large L REGARDLESS OF s. If a minimum exists for both signs, existence does not
depend on the parametrization and ELEC-071's exposure is reduced to a statement
about the minimum's location and depth rather than about whether the object
exists.

## Locked bars
B1 Test both signs numerically across parameter choices. Report the stationary
   points and their stability for each.
B2 IF EXISTENCE HOLDS FOR BOTH: state precisely what DOES still depend on the
   sign, and do not overstate the reduction.
B3 THE BINDING-ENERGY QUESTION, which the sign genuinely controls: report
   E(L*) for both signs and state what a positive versus negative value means
   here, given that both limits L -> 0 and L -> infinity send E to +infinity.
B4 HONESTY: Derrick scaling varies ONE parameter, the width, at fixed profile
   and fixed amplitude. It cannot see instabilities in other directions, and a
   minimum under scaling is not a proof of stability. State this.
