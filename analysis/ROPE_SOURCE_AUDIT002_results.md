# ROPE-SOURCE-AUDIT-002 — Undriven circulation and nontrivial holonomy

## Question

Can an undriven, closed rope possess a stable static or persistent dynamical circulation that generates a derived, non-integer observable holonomy?

## Locked null hypothesis

In the absence of imposed motion, torque, current, flux, pumping, or an explicit circulation constraint, circulation relaxes to zero or the only protected phase is an integer multiple of 2 pi, which is spectrally trivial in the validated Aharonov--Bohm instrument.

## Corpus audit

### 1. Static handedness supplies orientation, not motion

`analysis/stiffness_and_circulation.py` establishes that a helix has a sign-definite screw-sense pseudovector and can therefore select the *sense* of a circulating response. Its own scope statement is explicit: the calculation explains why circulation is possible and of the right type/sign, but it is not an equation of motion and supplies no drive or magnitude.

A static helical geometry is therefore not a persistent current.

### 2. The screw mechanism requires prescribed rotation

EM-014 (`benchmarks/em/screw_current.py`) verifies that a rotating helix transports one unit of linking per turn and that the steady current obeys

\[
I=\frac{\omega}{2\pi}.
\]

The angular rate `omega` is supplied to the benchmark. In the undriven case, `omega=0`, so the mechanism predicts `I=0`. EM-014 is registered as a mechanical realization of an already-prescribed transported-linking current, not as spontaneous current generation.

### 3. The continuity benchmark presupposes a pump and return path

EM-008 (`benchmarks/em/current_continuity.py`) demonstrates that a balanced source and sink on a closed loop can sustain bounded circulating transport, whereas an unbalanced source winds up the rope. The nonzero current exists because the model explicitly inserts a pump and return. The benchmark derives the continuity condition, not an undriven source.

### 4. The Maxwell-like field dynamics take current as input

EM-010 evolves a supplied source current `J` through a modeled field equation. The source term is not generated by the static rope state. Consequently it cannot establish self-starting or persistent circulation.

### 5. No energetic or topological protection of angular rate is derived

The corpus contains conserved configuration invariants such as linking and defect winding. It does not contain an invariant that quantizes or protects the *rate* of transport. A circulation rate can be reduced continuously,

\[
\omega \rightarrow 0,
\]

without changing the rope's integer linking number. Conservation of configuration topology therefore does not imply persistence of motion.

No stable or metastable undriven circulating stationary state, barrier against relaxation, or spontaneous time-reversal-breaking ground state is derived in the current action or energy corpus.

### 6. The only derived phase is spectrally trivial

FND-013 supplies the exact integer circulation

\[
\oint d\theta=2\pi N,\qquad N\in\mathbb Z.
\]

The validated AB instrument satisfies

\[
E_n(\theta+2\pi)=E_n(\theta).
\]

Therefore

\[
E_n(2\pi N)=E_n(0).
\]

The corpus's natural derived topological phase lies exactly in the spectrally trivial class. This is a structural obstruction, not merely a missing numerical estimate.

### 7. Every apparent escape adds a new postulate

A nontrivial result would require at least one of the following: fractional effective coupling, fractional or offset flux, a protected compact phase, spontaneous persistent current, or an explicit linking-dependent action term. None is derived. Introducing one would be a new mechanism/postulate and cannot be counted as completion of the present branch.

## Locked-bar outcome

| Bar | Result |
|---|---|
| No external drive | **FAIL** — all current mechanisms use prescribed rotation, pump, or source current |
| Dynamical selection | **FAIL** — no existing action selects nonzero circulation |
| Stable/metastable viability | **FAIL** — no protected nonzero rate or circulating minimum is derived |
| Derived source map to q Phi / hbar | **FAIL** |
| Non-integer holonomy | **FAIL** — the derived value is 2 pi N |
| Magnitude above instrument uncertainty | **FAIL / not calculable** |
| No arbitrary tuning | **PASS only for the null result** |

## Finding

**NO_UNDRIVEN_NONTRIVIAL_PERSISTENT_HOLONOMY**

## Structural obstruction

**INTEGER_WINDING_HOLONOMY_SPECTRALLY_TRIVIAL**

The current framework supplies neither an undriven persistent circulation nor a non-integer observable phase. More sharply, the one topological phase it genuinely derives is exactly invisible to the validated AB spectrum.

## Branch decision

The physical Aharonov--Bohm branch is **closed as unsupported under the current framework**.

ROPE-VALIDATION-001 through 004 remain valid instrument work, but their programme status is:

> **Validated gauge instrument with no nontrivial internally sourced target.**

The branch may be reopened only by introducing and independently justifying a new degree of freedom or action term that derives a stable non-integer holonomy and its magnitude. Such work must be registered from inception as a new postulate or mechanism, not as a refinement of the closed branch.

## Methodological lesson

The corpus should require a source-and-scale audit before building a new numerical instrument. Both the pilot-wave and gauge-holonomy branches developed functioning machinery before establishing that the framework supplied the quantity the machinery required.


## Obstruction provenance (added on review)

Not all the obstructions above carry the same weight, and a future reader
deciding whether to reopen this branch should know which is which.

**DERIVED obstructions** (positive results that foreclose a family):

- *Integer winding is spectrally trivial.* FND-013 derives 2 pi N; the
  validated instrument is 2 pi periodic; therefore E_n(2 pi N) = E_n(0)
  exactly. This is a theorem-level block, not a missing estimate.
- *Twist stiffens without gapping.* The twist energy depends on the gradient
  (du/ds)^2 rather than the displacement u, so long-wavelength relaxation
  remains available and no derived mass term pins a circulating phase. This
  forecloses the whole metastable-barrier family in one stroke.

**ABSENCES** (nothing currently registered, which is weaker):

- No invariant quantizing or protecting the *rate* of transport. Section 5's
  argument that omega can relax continuously at fixed Lk is correct but is a
  statement about what the corpus lacks, not a proof that no such invariant
  exists.
- No metastable undriven circulating minimum identified.

THE SOFT JOINT IS THE RATE-PROTECTION ABSENCE. Any reopening should be
expected to push there first, and must additionally clear the gapless-twist
obstruction explicitly rather than reaching for twist again.
