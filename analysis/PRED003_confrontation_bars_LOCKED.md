# PRED-003 confrontation — locked bars (before the arithmetic)

## Commission
PRED-003 predicts d ln alpha = -2 d ln G. Both rates are measured today with
central values, signs and uncertainties. This is a direct consistency test,
not a bound comparison, and needs no new instrument.

## Data, fixed before computing (published, retrieved 2026-08-01)
D1 alpha-dot/alpha = 1.0(1.1)e-18 /yr — Yb+ E3/E2 optical clock comparison,
   PTB (Filzinger et al. 2023). Tighter variant from the same programme's
   supplemental linear-drift fit: 1.8(2.5)e-19 /yr. Both reported.
D2 Gdot/G = 7.1(7.6)e-14 /yr — lunar laser ranging, Hofmann & Muller,
   Class. Quantum Grav. 35, 035015 (2018). Independent older determination
   (4 +/- 9)e-13 /yr (Williams, Turyshev & Boggs 2004) reported as a check.

## Locked verdict rules
B1 FORWARD TEST. Predict alpha-dot/alpha = -2 (Gdot/G) from D2 and compare to
   D1. Report the tension in sigma of the COMBINED uncertainty. CONSISTENT if
   under 2 sigma, TENSION if 2-3, REFUTED above 3.
B2 INVERSE TEST. Predict Gdot/G = -(1/2)(alpha-dot/alpha) from D1 and compare
   to current G sensitivity. Report how far below present reach it falls.
B3 THE CHANNEL TEST, and it must not be oversold. PRED-003's benchmark shows
   drift through the strand spacing gives +1 rather than -2. Ask whether the
   data can currently distinguish the channels. IF BOTH MEASUREMENTS ARE
   CONSISTENT WITH ZERO, THE RATIO OF CENTRAL VALUES IS MEANINGLESS and must
   be reported as undetermined, NOT as a preferred channel.
B4 THE FALSIFICATION CONDITION. State what future measurement kills it:
   the Gdot/G detection significance at which the implied alpha drift becomes
   incompatible with D1.
B5 HONESTY. A null-vs-null consistency is WEAK evidence. Say so. Passing this
   test is not confirmation; it is survival.
