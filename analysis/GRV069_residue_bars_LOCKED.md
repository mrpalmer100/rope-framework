# GRV-069 — COLLECTIVE_ROTATION_MODE_UNSCREENED: the correction and the residue test. Locked bars

## Commission
An external reviewer accepted GRV-068's structural finding and rejected its
verdict. Three corrections are owed and are executed here, followed by an
attempt at the test the reviewer specified.

## The corrections owed
C1 "STEP 4 PASSES" IS TOO STRONG. A vanishing determinant at k = 0 does not
   prove a localized angular-momentum source produces a long-range field. The
   decisive object is the POLE RESIDUE (O_g^dagger v0)(v0^dagger S_J). A zero
   mode can exist and stay invisible if the source is orthogonal to it, if the
   metric observable projects only onto the massive mode, if derivative
   couplings cancel the 1/k^2 pole, or if the zero mode is a rigid global
   symmetry rather than a locally excitable field.
C2 THE (2,1) EIGENVECTOR IS COORDINATE-DEPENDENT. It varies with field
   normalisation, with whether u denotes displacement or a rescaled rotational
   variable, with kinetic coefficients and with Fourier conventions. The
   invariant statement is that the gapless mode has VANISHING RELATIVE
   ROTATION: eta = phi - (1/2) curl u = 0.
C3 "ANGULAR MOMENTUM IS TOTAL ROTATION THEREFORE IT EXCITES THE MASSLESS MODE"
   IS AN INFERENCE, NOT A DERIVATION. A source carrying J could couple to
   macrorotation, microrotation, their difference, a curl, or a parity-odd
   combination. The source term must be written and its coefficients derived.
