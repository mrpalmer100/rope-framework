# ELEC-077 — Which energy functional is physical? Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-076 found two functionals carried as one, differing by k/T0 >= 1.9e8, and
named the decider: does the medium shorten at constant tension (pure-tension
form) or resist with stiffness k (eliminate-u form)? That is constitutive
content, not algebra.

## What each functional presupposes, fixed before deciding
PURE TENSION, E = T0 (sqrt(1 + psi'^2) - 1): the energy is tension times the
ARC-LENGTH EXCESS of the strand's curve over the straight reference. It refers
only to the curve's geometry in space. It is manifestly invariant under
reparametrization, because arc length is.
ELIMINATE-U, E = (k/2) eps^2 + T0 eps with eps = sqrt((1+u')^2 + psi'^2) - 1 and
u' minimised: this treats u as a PHYSICAL DISPLACEMENT FIELD carrying elastic
energy, i.e. it presupposes that there are material points which can be
displaced longitudinally and whose displacement costs energy.

## The registered claim that decides it
FND-REL-002 (Derived): strand mechanics forbid the Galilean convective term
because NO MATERIAL VELOCITY EXISTS. EM-RECON-011 leg (1) draws the consequence
explicitly: longitudinal displacement u is GAUGE-LIKE because strands have NO
MATERIAL POINTS.

## Locked bars
B1 State the presupposition of each functional and check it against FND-REL-002.
B2 THE VERDICT, whichever way it falls, including the possibility that the
   framework's own claims are in tension with each other.
B3 CONSEQUENCE for ELEC-068/069/070 and for EM-RECON-011, stated without
   softening. If EM-RECON-011 is itself internally strained, say so -- it both
   asserts u is gauge AND computes couplings from u's elastic energy.
B4 CONSEQUENCE for the exact line ELEC-073/074/075.
B5 HONESTY: this is an argument from a registered claim, not a new computation.
   Its force is exactly the force of FND-REL-002 and of the reading applied.
