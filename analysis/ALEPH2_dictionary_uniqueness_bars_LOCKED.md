# COMMISSION ALEPH-2 -- EM-016'S BLOCKER (iv): IS THE DICTIONARY UNIQUE? (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before any argument is constructed. EM-016 is Modeled
rather than Derived for FOUR stated reasons. This commission attacks the
one that is a THEOREM rather than an input:*

  (iv) the identification's UNIQUENESS -- that no other mechanical
       assignment reproduces the same phenomenology -- is not established.

*The other three are NOT in scope and may not be argued around:
(i) SIGMA's absolute value is a measured input; (ii) EM-010's inertial
term is assumed; (iii) phi's channel identification (EM-RECON-011) is
Modeled. A win here retires ONE blocker and does not by itself change
EM-016's grade -- the commission must say so explicitly whatever it
finds.*

## What must be proved (stated precisely before attempting it)

Let a MECHANICAL ASSIGNMENT be any map from the rope's mechanical state
to a pair of fields (E', B') on spacetime. Two assignments are
PHENOMENOLOGICALLY EQUIVALENT if they predict the same force on every
test winding, at every point, for every test velocity.

**Claim to be tested:** the registered dictionary's (E, B) is unique
among mechanical assignments up to a transformation that changes no
observable -- i.e. any phenomenologically equivalent assignment differs
from it by at most (a) a gauge transformation of the potentials and
(b) a single global constant rescaling absorbed into the calibration
SIGMA.

## The registered inputs the proof may use (closed list)

- q = winding/linking number, integer-quantized (GG-006, EM-001;
  Derived). In particular the charge group is Z and there are NO
  magnetic monopoles registered -- windings are the only charge.
- The operational definition already in registered use: E = force per
  winding (EM-015, EM-RECON-024's stated definition).
- The Lorentz force law q(E + v x B) from gauge-forced coupling
  (EM-013, Derived).
- Maxwell from Bianchi + Chern-Weil in d=3 (EM-003, Derived).

## What would DEFEAT the claim (pre-committed, so the test can fail)

- A DUALITY freedom: if (E, B) -> (E cos t + B sin t, B cos t - E sin t)
  left all forces invariant, the assignment would NOT be unique. The
  commission must CHECK this rather than assume it, and must state what
  registered fact (if any) breaks it.
- A velocity-degeneracy: if forces at all velocities failed to separate
  E from B, B would be underdetermined. Must be checked by explicit
  construction, not asserted.

## Verdict grammar (pre-committed)

- **UNIQUENESS-DERIVED**: the claim is proved from the closed list.
  Blocker (iv) retires; EM-016's ledger drops to three, grade unchanged,
  and the remaining three are restated as the live obstacles.
- **UNIQUENESS-FAILS**: a genuine alternative assignment exists. This is
  a MORE valuable outcome than a win and must be registered at full
  volume, since it would mean the dictionary is a choice presented as a
  mapping.
- **UNDERSPECIFIED**: the registered inputs do not settle it.

## Standing rule

No blocker other than (iv) may be argued away here, and the commission
may not upgrade EM-016. Grade changes on the strength of a partial
discharge would be exactly the bar-shopping the corpus refuses.
