## WHY NOW
LEAD-RAD closed the alpha residual (+178.8 ppm) and the Schwinger-class
g-2 gap (-1160 ppm) at the quantum fence on 9 Aug because "no
registered alpha-independent cutoff exists." The fine level did not
exist then. Between 12 and 17 Aug the corpus registered a whole family
of fine lengths: the a_f ceiling (FND-083/087), the winding radii
R_1 = 0.22508 a_f and R_2 (FND-125/126), the fibre radius bound
r_s <= 0.1874 a_f (FND-131), the pitch p = a_f at worst case, and the
suspension rule itself (FND-110). Nobody has asked whether any of them
is the regulator LEAD-RAD named. Separately, GRV-128 reduced the
frame-dragging gate from {(G I_p)_f, a_f} to {a_f} alone, and
Prediction 33 tied the PeV rotation quantum to the same a_f. One
number now sits under three sectors and one fenced residual. This
commission does not guess it; it audits what could fix it and runs
the one computation the fence pre-authorized.

## WHAT IS NOT ASSUMED
No value of a_f, m or n_sub in Legs 1-4. No new length, no new
coupling, no new regulator: the only regulator admitted in Q3 is a
length Q1 certifies clean, and if Q1 certifies none, Q3 does not run.
LEAD-RAD's bars are inherited verbatim: alpha out of the room during
construction; derived coefficients only; no separate freedom between

close to a plausible regulator is not a regulator without a
dependency path.

## DESIGN COMMITMENTS (locked at lock)
D1. THE PROVENANCE TABLE (Q1) is built by symbol inspection of the
    registry and the benchmark sources, one row per length, each row
    carrying: the defining relation; every input it descends from,
    to the leaf; the leaf classification (measured / granted /
    derived / calibration); whether alpha (CODATA or the chain's own
    output), m_e, or the LHAASO anchor appears anywhere in the
    chain; and VALUE / CEILING / RATIO. The table is frozen before
    Q2 begins. Rows at lock (the list is closed; a length found
    missing is added by amendment with its reason): a (M-point),
    a_f (ceiling), p, R_1, R_2, r_s (bound and derive-point), l_chi,
    w, R* (chain), r_min (W-solver, ruled out by LEAD-RAD and
    re-listed for completeness), the electron size 0.407 fm
    (ELEC-052/081, lattice-anchored), the hollow-core r0 (ELEC-074/
    075/090, listed with ELEC-075's continuum-failure verdict on
    its face).
D2. THE CLEAN RULE. A length is alpha-CLEAN iff no leaf in its chain
    is alpha or a quantity computed from alpha; m_e-loading does NOT
    disqualify (LEAD-RAD's condition is alpha-independence, and the
    chain already owns m_e through the M-point). The LHAASO anchor
    is a measured leaf and does not disqualify; it makes the length
    a CEILING suspended on m, and the charter treats ceilings as
    ceilings.
D3. THE INVENTORY (Q2) is a closed list of relations, each written
    as f(a_f, other unknowns) = measured quantity, with the other
    unknowns named: the frame-dragging amplitude (chi remains), the
    rotation quantum (a probe remains), the photon ceiling (m
    remains), the Casimir coefficient (the per-mode action remains,
    F3), the LEAD-RAD A2 sum (no other unknown if Q1 supplies the
    regulator), the n_sub packing relation (n_sub remains), and any
    relation Q1's tracing surfaces. A PAIR closes iff two relations
    share a_f and no other unknown, with both measured sides in the
    registry.
D4. Q3, IF LICENSED. The A2 reactive sum is evaluated as a function
    of M* on the registered kernel (reused byte-identically), then
    M* = R* / r_core with r_core the certified length as a symbol in
    a_f; the moment-side weight w2(a_f) is formed. The energy-side
    weight w1 is carried as LEAD-RAD left it (finite, normalization
    unfixed) and the arbiter's single-mechanism requirement is
    applied: the same regulator and the same coupling normalization
    must serve P1 and P2. If P1 cannot be formed because w1's
    normalization is still unregistered, that is reported as the
    reason and P2 alone is confronted as LEAD-RAD's own registrable
    "derived P2 with P1 pending" outcome.
D5. THE CEILING CASE. If Q1 yields only a clean CEILING (a_f <=
    a_f,ceil, r_core proportional to a_f), Q3 is run as a BOUND: w2
    is evaluated across a_f in (0, a_f,ceil] and the confrontation
    asks whether any a_f in the interval lands the targets. A bound
    is not a pin; no exposure clause arms on it (the GRV-113 rule).
D6. NUMBERS ATTACH LAST. Legs 1-4 are symbolic and reading; Leg 5
    loads the targets once.

  GATE-PAIR-CLOSES: two registered relations share a_f and no other

  GATE-UNDERDETERMINED: every relation carries a second unknown. The

Q3 (exactly one, only if licensed):
  RAD-TWO-LANDINGS: one a_f (value, or a point inside the ceiling)
      lands P2 within the Schwinger coefficient's stated tolerance
      AND P1 within +/- 0.4 ppm of +178.8, under one normalization.
      LEAD-RAD's GRADUATION outcome is reached at the fence's own
      re-opening condition; a_f is PINNED TWICE by two residuals it
      never touched (this is not a calibration spend: two targets,

      Lambda_nat becomes a value (GRV-128/FND-122 chain), the PeV
      rotation quantum becomes a value (Prediction 33), and n_sub
      follows from the packing relation.
  RAD-ONE-LANDING: exactly one of P1 / P2 lands at some a_f inside
      the admissible interval. The a_f that lands it is a
      CALIBRATION CANDIDATE, declared as such, and the other target's
      miss is recorded with its size. Registrable as LEAD-RAD's own
      "derived P2 with P1 pending" (or the converse) at exactly that
      status, no higher. The fence stays half-open.
  RAD-EXCLUDED: no a_f in the admissible interval lands either
      target. The radiative back-reaction with THIS regulator is
      refuted as the residual's mechanism; the fence re-closes on a
      stronger footing (a computed exclusion, not an absence of
      regulator). Failed and kept.
  RAD-NOT-LICENSED: Q1 returned GATE-LOADED. Q3 not run; the
      worklog's go-decision remains named and unopened.

Anything else (a control fails; a row's chain cannot be traced to a
leaf; w2 does not converge at the regulated M*): NO CALL; report and
stop.

## NO-RESCUE RULE
No length is added to the table after Q2 begins except by amendment
with reason. No relaxation of the CLEAN rule to admit a loaded length
because it is the only candidate. No re-normalization of w1 to make
P1 land. No second regulator. The a_f that lands one target is not
carried into the other target's evaluation as a free choice; it is
carried as the same number, and the second target reports what it
reports.
