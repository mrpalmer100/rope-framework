## THE SCOPE TEST, fixed at lock (B2)
For each of the three, the annotation must separate, in this order:
(a) WHAT IS SUPERSEDED -- the specific proposition that was true of
    the registered action and is no longer true of the action as it
    now stands with L_C3 in it;
(b) WHAT SURVIVES UNTOUCHED -- the propositions that are statements
    about the lambda = 0 action, or about structure L_C3 does not
    touch, and therefore remain correct as registered;
(c) WHAT IS UNCHANGED IN PRACTICE -- the consequence, if any, that
    GRV-113's cap leaves standing at the measured magnitude.
A claim whose content is entirely (b) is annotated CONTEXT-ONLY and is
NOT marked superseded. Marking a claim superseded that was not is as
much a corpus error as failing to mark one that was.
