*Locked 2026-08-11, before searching for answers. An external reviewer
challenged figures/electron_winding.png: the rendered core (a solid

  If the electron is a winding defect in the phase/orientation of an

## The three questions, answered separately and by claim name

- **Z1 IS phi REGISTERED?** Does a registered claim identify the
  circle-valued variable whose winding is the charge, as a MECHANICAL
  degree of freedom rather than an abstract phase? Name it and its grade,
  or report that the reviewer is right that it is unspecified.
- **Z2 IS THE r = 0 SINGULARITY REGULARIZED?** A winding phi = theta is
  undefined at the origin. Does a registered claim say what the corpus's
  solution does there? Report the mechanism and its grade, or report that
  nothing does.
- **Z3 WHAT OCCUPIES THE CORE REGION?** If Z2 returns a finite-radius
  structure, is its INTERIOR registered? Distinguish sharply between
  "the field terminates at a boundary" (a statement about the exterior
  solution) and "here is what the strands do inside" (a statement about
  the interior). The commission may NOT let the first pass for the
  second.
