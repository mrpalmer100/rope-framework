# ELEC-061 — Formal retirement of the mesoscopic-hbar sector: locked bars

## Commission
Six independent closures now bear on the mesoscopic-hbar identification
(NUCQ-001 nuclear data; ELEC-057 the Lorentz bound and all strand scales;
ELEC-058 bundling; ELEC-059 unit width; ELEC-060 two media), the last four
reached with no new experimental input. The corpus should say so in its
registry rather than leaving the sector open with escapes enumerated.

## The discipline this pass must observe
RETIREMENT IS NOT DEMOLITION. The claim to be closed is the IDENTIFICATION of
hbar's action length with a mesoscopic patch of the medium. Several claims in
the HBAR chain are independent of that identification and several are
NEGATIVE results whose value does not depend on it at all. Over-retiring
would destroy kept failures, which is the opposite of this corpus's practice.

## Locked classification rule (applied before any status is changed)
Each claim in HBAR-001..010 and the NUCQ chain is assigned exactly one of:
- CLOSED: its content depends on the mesoscopic identification being true.
- SURVIVES: its content is length-independent, or is a NEGATIVE/kept failure
  whose lesson stands regardless (a mechanism that failed to select a scale
  fails whether or not the scale was ever viable).
- ALREADY FAILED: registered Failed already; no action but annotation.
The rule is mechanical: a claim SURVIVES if removing the mesoscopic patch
leaves its stated result intact.

## Locked bars
B1 Classify every claim by the rule above and report the counts.
B2 NO STATUS DOWNGRADE for any claim classified SURVIVES. State explicitly
   which negatives are being preserved and why.
B3 The standing-wave FORM (HBAR-001, S = pi T A^2/(2c), L cancels) must be
   checked for length-independence directly, not assumed. If it survives, the
   retirement is of the identification only and must be described that way.
B4 File a single sector-level closure record that a future reader hits before
   any individual HBAR claim, listing the six closures and what remains live.
B5 Report what the corpus LOSES: name the predictions withdrawn.
