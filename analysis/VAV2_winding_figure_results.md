obviously the same object:

  number of an internal circle-valued orientation; ropes need not be
  displaced at all. This is what the figure draws, and it rests on a
  Derived symmetry theorem.

  with a clasp radius, a hard core, shape ratios (18.3:1 asymmetry,

the field winding terminates, which is how the figure's legend reads
("electron core = terminus of the twist field") -- but **no registered
claim establishes that identification.** The corpus has never adjudicated
whether the clasp geometry and the field winding are one object seen two
ways, or two competing models.

That adjudication is a commission, not a caption. It is named here as an
acquisition target: **derive the clasp-and-loop geometry as the core of
the GRV-020 winding, or register the two pictures as competing.**
