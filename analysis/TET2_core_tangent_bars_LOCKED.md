# COMMISSION TET-2 -- THE CORE'S TANGENT FIELD (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before computing. ELEC-090 left exactly one thing
undetermined: the tangential direction field on the core sphere, whose
zeros must have total index 2. This commission attempts to determine it
by MATCHING to the registered exterior, rather than by an energetic
principle (which would require an interior model the corpus lacks).*

## The matching question

The exterior is a winding of GRV-020's internal azimuth with winding
number n = +-1 about an axis. The core boundary carries a tangent field
whose zeros total index 2 (ELEC-090). Does the exterior winding SELECT
among the admissible tangent fields?

## The checks

- **T1 THE AXISYMMETRIC SOLUTION.** Under axisymmetry -- already a
  registered assumption of the ELEC-073/074 profile construction, and
  stated as such -- determine which tangent field on S^2 matches an
  n = 1 exterior winding. Compute its zero set and the index at each
  zero. Verify the total is 2 (a consistency check on ELEC-090, which
  must be reported whether or not it passes).
- **T2 IS THE ALTERNATIVE EXCLUDED?** Hairy-ball permits one index-2
  zero as well as two index-1 zeros. Determine whether axisymmetry plus
  the n = 1 matching excludes the single-zero option, or whether both
  survive. Do not assume the symmetric answer.
- **T3 THE CONSEQUENCE FOR ANGULAR MOMENTUM -- and this check is
  mandatory whatever it returns.** If the core's tangent field
  circulates, its SENSE is fixed by the sign of n. Determine whether the
  corpus is thereby committed to charge sign determining circulation
  sense. If it is, confront that against the experimental fact that
  electrons of a single charge occur with BOTH spin orientations. A
  framework that predicts charge fixes spin is falsified; this must be
  computed and reported at full volume, not avoided.

## Verdict grammar (pre-committed)

- **FIELD-DETERMINED**: T1 and T2 succeed; the core's tangent field is
  fixed and its zero structure named.
- **FIELD-UNDERDETERMINED**: both admissible options survive.
- **AND SEPARATELY**, T3 returns one of:
  - **SPIN-INDEPENDENT**: the circulation is not the spin, with the
    registered reason;
  - **SPIN-CONFLICT**: the corpus is committed to charge fixing spin.
    This is a FALSIFICATION-SHAPED finding and is registered
    Failed-and-kept if it holds, with no attempt to rescue it in the
    same commission.
