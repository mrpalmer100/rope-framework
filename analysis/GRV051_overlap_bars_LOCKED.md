GRV-050 showed the ratchet source is purely deviatoric with 40% TT content.
GRV-025 showed the weave band's IR-universal induced action carries the
Einstein-Hilbert tensor pattern, responding in h_xy with (xx, zz, xy, xz) =

of magnitude and the diagonal and xz channels are suppressed. Joining them is
the last step between the whisper and a LIGO search specification.

## The coupling, fixed before computing
The source enters the action as S_int ~ (1/2) h_ij Delta_sigma_ij, so the
amplitude excited in a channel with basis tensor E is proportional to the
projection <E, Delta_sigma>, and the POWER to its square weighted by the
channel's measured response. GRV-025's measured responses ARE the weights.

## The geometry, fixed before computing
GRV-050 established Delta_sigma = diag(-cos 2theta, +cos 2theta, 0) in the
crossing frame -- pure "plus" polarization in the plane of the crossing. The
crossing's ORIENTATION relative to the lattice axes is arbitrary and must be
averaged over SO(3): a reconnection shell has no preferred alignment with the
weave's cubic frame.
