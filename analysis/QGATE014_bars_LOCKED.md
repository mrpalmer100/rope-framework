# QGATE-014 — Born-relaxation bars (locked before data)

Protocol: 2-D unit square; 16-mode superposition; ensemble sampled from the ground-state density but guided by the excited superposition; coarse-grained H and L1 meters. Control: one stationary real mode under the identical integrator.

Bars:

1. H must fall by more than 50%.
2. L1 distance to |Psi|² must fall by at least 50%.
3. The single-mode control must show less than 15% fractional change in H.

Interpretive limit fixed in advance: passing demonstrates relaxation in this mode-mixing example. It does not prove universal attraction, derive Psi from rope mechanics, or establish the Born measure for every state.
