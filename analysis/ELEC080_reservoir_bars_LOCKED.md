# ELEC-080 — Is the reservoir premise defensible? Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-079 selected the pure-tension functional by two independent routes but
rested it on a premise: an infinite PRE-TENSIONED medium acting as a reservoir,
supplying length to a local disturbance at fixed tension T0, with an
instantaneous longitudinal channel so the supply is not delayed. It named that
premise as the line's one remaining open item.

## The test, fixed before computing
Compare the two self-consistent pictures:
  A. RESERVOIR: tension maintained at T0 by the far field; local energy is
     T0 x (local excess length).
  B. CLOSED SYSTEM: no reservoir, total length fixed, so the T0 eps term
     integrates to zero globally and only the elastic term (k/2) INT eps^2
     survives.
Work out what picture B implies for the medium's transverse response, and check
it against something the corpus already claims independently of the electron
work.

## Locked bars
B1 In picture B, is eps = 0 achievable pointwise? If so the minimum energy is
   exactly zero and the medium absorbs transverse displacement free of charge.
B2 THE CONSEQUENCE for transverse waves: c = sqrt(T0/mu) requires a MAINTAINED
   tension. State whether picture B supports wave propagation.
B3 THE VERDICT: if picture B forbids light, then the reservoir premise is not
   an extra assumption imported for the electron work but a REQUIREMENT of the
   framework's own optics. Report it either way.
B4 THE REMAINING EXPOSURE: what does the reservoir premise itself require, and
   is anything in the corpus in tension with it?
B5 HONESTY: state what this does NOT establish.
