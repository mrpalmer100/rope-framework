# COMMISSION TAV -- UV-MODE-001: IS LIGHT NYQUIST-LIMITED BY a? (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before any adjudication. Chartered on an external
reviewer's challenge, adopted with attribution: FND-060 proved a cutoff
for the coarse DISPLACEMENT field; the reviewer asks whether that
variable is rigorously identical to the physical radiation degree of
freedom at high frequency, or whether the no-go has been applied to the
wrong variable. The challenge attacks the premise rather than the
arithmetic, which is the right place, and it is cheap to settle. This
commission may not adopt any grant, and may not weaken FND-060's theorem
within its own scope.*

## The precise question

FND-060 discretized the transverse displacement field s(x_i, t) sampled
at crossings of spacing a, and bounded omega_max ~ c/a for that field.
Is the physical electromagnetic radiation variable IDENTICAL to that
sampled displacement at k ~ 1/a, or is it a distinct object (phase,
connection, topological or collective variable) whose Fourier support is
not limited to the first Brillouin zone?

## What must be established (each separately, on the record)

- **A1 THE IDENTIFICATION AUDIT.** What does the registry say light IS?
  Cite the claim and its grade. If the identification is to a mechanical
  displacement, state whether that displacement is defined ONLY at
  crossings (site-sampled, hence Nyquist-limited) or CONTINUOUSLY along
  strands (in which case FND-059's anisotropy result governs, not
  FND-060's).
- **A2 IS A DISTINCT RADIATION VARIABLE REGISTERED?** Sweep for a gauge
  field, connection, phase variable, or vector potential registered as
  the PRIMITIVE radiation degree of freedom rather than as a derived
  dictionary entry. If one exists, FND-060's scope is narrower than
  stated and the escape is OPEN. If none exists, say so plainly.
- **A3 THE DERIVED-VARIABLE TEST.** If the corpus's E and B are DERIVED
  FROM the displacement (a dictionary built on s), then their Fourier
  content is inherited from s and no independent UV behaviour is
  available -- a function of a band-limited field is band-limited in the
  same sense. State whether the registered dictionary is of that form.
- **A4 THE REVIEWER'S OPTION 7 (emergent a).** Is a registered as a
  fundamental constituent spacing or as a collective/correlation length?
  Cite the provenance claim. If a is emergent, the escape survives at
  the price of naming the deeper constituent scale.

## Verdict grammar (pre-committed)

- **NYQUIST-CONFIRMED**: light is the site-sampled displacement or a
  derived function of it; FND-060 applies as stated; substructure (or a
  new carrier) is required, and the reviewer's cheap escape is closed.
- **SCOPE-NARROWED**: a distinct registered radiation variable exists
  whose UV support is not set by a; FND-060 is correct for the coarse
  mechanical branch but does not bound light. The escape is OPEN and
  must be chartered.
- **UNDERSPECIFIED**: the registry does not determine which variable
  radiates at high k. This is itself a finding -- the corpus would then
  be unable to say what light IS in the UV, and that gap must be
  registered rather than resolved by preference.

## Standing rule

If the audit returns NYQUIST-CONFIRMED, the commission must say so even
though the alternative would be more convenient, and must NOT propose
substructure (naming a primitive is the author's).
