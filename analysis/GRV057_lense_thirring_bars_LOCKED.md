## The question that must be answered honestly, and not assumed
Does the framework predict mu = 0? GRV-056 shows the WAVE OPERATOR cannot carry
a shift. But GRV-026 asserts COVARIANT MATTER SOURCING, and a covariant source
is the full stress-energy including the momentum density T_0i -- which is
exactly what sources gravitomagnetism in GR. THESE TWO CANNOT BOTH BE
UNRESTRICTED. Bars B2 and B3 must establish which the corpus actually has,
rather than choosing the convenient one.
