## Commission

VECTOR SECTOR; that the linearised operator cannot carry a shift; and that the
only surviving escape is higher order, since gravitomagnetism in GR is generated
by the nonlinearity. It named this as the corpus's highest-stakes computation.

## The construction, fixed before computing

truncation, expand it around a background configuration, and read the quadratic
form for perturbations. If a mixed d_t d_a term appears, the vector sector
exists and is generated by the nonlinearity exactly as in GR.
