magnitude on their registered faces (EM-RECON-014's SIGMA ~ 1e25 J/m^3;
FND-030's pinned Sigma_eff ~ 3.6e35 J/m^3). Compute the comparison
explicitly. Three outcomes are pre-committed:

  registered conversion). Then blocker (i) is ALREADY substantially
  discharged by FND-030/034 and nobody propagated it to EM-016 -- a

  disambiguation plus a corrected blocker statement.

  the ambiguity itself is the finding and the relabel must WAIT.
