# COMMISSION CHET-2 -- SPECIFYING THE HOLLOW'S INTERIOR (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before any computation. The target ELEC-089 and
ELEC-088 named from opposite directions: ELEC-074 established that the
electron's exterior field exists only outside r0 = sqrt(C), and NOTHING
registered says what the strands do inside. This commission attempts a
specification.*

## The standard this must meet, stated before attempting it

A specification is NOT a plausible picture. It must be FORCED by the
registered exterior solution and the registered mechanics -- the same
standard EM-018 met by elimination and EM-019 met by symmetry. If the
best available result is a CONSTRAINT on the interior rather than a
determination of it, that must be registered as a constraint and NOT
dressed as a solution.

## The three questions

- **C1 WHAT IS THE BOUNDARY CONDITION, GEOMETRICALLY?** The first
  integral is r^2 F(p) = C with F(p) = p/sqrt(1+p^2) and p = psi' the
  transverse slope. Determine what F = 1 means as a STATEMENT ABOUT THE
  STRANDS, not as an algebraic limit. If p is a slope, F has a
  trigonometric reading; compute it and state the physical configuration
  at r0.
- **C2 IS THE HARD CORE KINEMATIC OR DYNAMICAL?** Determine whether the
  non-existence of solutions inside r0 follows from a BOUND THAT CANNOT
  BE EXCEEDED (kinematics/geometry) or from an energetic accident that
  a different parameter choice could move. State which, and what that
  implies about how universal the hollow is.
- **C3 WHAT DOES THE BOUNDARY CONDITION FORCE ABOUT THE INTERIOR?**
  Given C1's configuration at r0, determine what the strands CAN do
  inside. Distinguish sharply, per ELEC-089's rule: a constraint that
  excludes configurations is not the same as a determination that
  produces one.

## Verdict grammar (pre-committed)

- **INTERIOR-DERIVED**: the interior configuration is forced. Requires
  showing no other configuration satisfies the boundary condition and
  the registered mechanics.
- **INTERIOR-CONSTRAINED**: the boundary condition forces properties of
  the interior without determining it. Register the properties, name what
  is still missing.
- **INTERIOR-UNDERSPECIFIED**: registered inputs do not constrain it;
  a new primitive or a new measurement is required. Name which.
