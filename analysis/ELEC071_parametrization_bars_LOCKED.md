# ELEC-071 — Is the truncation physical? The parametrization test. Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-070 named the load-bearing uncertainty of the whole ELEC-067..070 line: the
exact elimination of the longitudinal field is degenerate, and everything rests
on a perturbative truncation whose validity is not established. This tests it.

## The test, fixed before computing
The transverse field can be expressed against two different base coordinates,
and the strain energy expands differently in each:
  A. the LAB coordinate x, with the medium free to stretch:
     E/T0 = sqrt(1 + psi_x^2) - 1
  B. ARCLENGTH s, the material coordinate of an inextensible medium, with the
     energy equal to tension times end-shortening:
     E/T0 = 1 - sqrt(1 - psi_s^2)
These describe the same physical string. If their quartic coefficients DIFFER IN
SIGN, then the attractive quartic driving the entire soliton line is a
PARAMETRIZATION ARTIFACT unless the framework independently selects one
coordinate, and the line is not physics until that selection is made.

## Locked bars
B1 Expand both and compare the quartic and sextic signs. Report the comparison
   whichever way it falls.
B2 IF THE SIGNS DIFFER: determine whether the corpus independently selects a
   parametrization. State the claim that does it, by ID, or report that none
   does -- in which case the soliton line is UNGROUNDED and must be labelled so.
B3 State what the selected parametrization implies for ELEC-067 through
   ELEC-070: which results survive, which are conditional, and on what.
B4 HONESTY: identifying the load-bearing claim is not the same as verifying it.
   If the whole line comes to rest on a single registered claim, say so and
   name the exposure.
