## P23 — the switch-on fingerprint (dark-count drift at constant temperature)

and relationships (rate declines; waiting times non-exponential;
temperature flat) but NO number: no epoch in seconds, no drift
magnitude, no committed functional form in laboratory units. The
absolute scale is open (FND-MATTER-003), so the transient's duration
could be anywhere from attoseconds to years. ELEC-062 admitted
sign-definite discrete outcomes (GRV-039's no-burst) as quantitative;

under scale-openness (an unobserved drift is always "outside the
window").

C2 DISTINCTIVE IN OBSERVABLE OUTCOME: FAILS, and this is the decisive
strike. Standard detector physics ALREADY predicts dark-count rate

afterpulsing from trapped-charge release, bias settling, and related

signature (elevated-then-decaying dark rate, non-exponential intervals,
temperature flat). The framework's distinctive content — a floor set by

cannot be discriminated without a committed timescale separating it from
trap physics, which returns to the C1 failure.

C3 CHECKABLE: in the weak sense yes (statistics campaigns on existing
hardware); in the required sense NO, because the discriminating branch
cannot be specified without the scale map.

C4 LIVE: passes. D1: passes (the kinetics chain FND-STRAND-008..021
touches no retired claim). D2: passes (detectors exist). D3: FAILS — the
discriminating branch ("drift persists at the medium-set epoch after
