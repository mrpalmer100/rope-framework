# GRV-060 — The twist route: does Gaede's twirling supply the missing sector? Locked bars

## Commission
GRV-059 registered the gravity sector Failed, concluding that nothing in the
action carries a stationary mass current. The operator objected that in Gaede's
picture ropes TWIRL at large scale as they do at small scale -- i.e. the medium
carries a twist degree of freedom that a rotating body could drive. That is a
concrete proposal and it must be tested rather than dismissed, because the
corpus DOES register a twist sector (FND-STRAND-002/003's Calugareanu ledger,
GRV-045's writhe exchange) that GRV-059's step-1 search did not consider.

## What the proposal must clear, from GRV-059's own list
R1 dependence on total angular momentum J
R2 correct 1/r^3 far-field behaviour
R3 vector/dipole angular structure, A_g ~ (J x r)/r^3
R4 the sign
R5 the numerical coefficient
R6 consistency with the existing static-gravity sector

## Locked bars
B1 Does a twist-density coupling produce a SHIFT, and is it STATIONARY? A shift
   sourced by a stationary twist needs no time dependence, which is the property
   GRV-058's mechanism lacked.
B2 What twist field does a rotating body produce in an elastic medium? Use the
   classical result, not an assumption. Check R2 and R3 explicitly.
B3 THE HONEST OBSTACLE, which must be stated and not skipped: a body rotating
   steadily in a medium rigidly attached to it winds up without bound, while a
   freely slipping body accumulates no twist. Determine what fixes the steady
   state and whether it introduces a free parameter.
B4 SCORE THE PROPOSAL against R1-R6. Report which are met, which are open, and
   which are not addressed. NO requirement may be scored met on structural
   plausibility alone.
B5 THE CONSEQUENCE FOR GRV-059: does its Failed status stand, need qualifying,
   or fall? State it either way.
