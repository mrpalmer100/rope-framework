# COMMISSION GIMEL-2 -- EM-016's BLOCKER (ii): IS THE INERTIAL TERM FORCED? (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before any argument is constructed. EM-016's ledger
stands at two after EM-017 and EM-018. This commission attacks the last
identification-shaped item: (ii) EM-010's kinetic term (mu/2)|da/dt|^2,
registered as ASSUMED -- "standard inertial dynamics, its form not
derived from strand mechanics."*

## The obstacle that must be faced first, not dodged

The obvious move -- "the strands have mass, so the kinetic energy is
Newtonian" -- is NOT available on its face, and the bar says so before
the attempt: FND-REL-002 (Derived) establishes there is NO material
velocity in this ontology, and EM-RECON-012 uses exactly that to FORBID
a mass term. A commission that writes down (mu/2)|dX/dt|^2 for material
points is contradicting two Derived claims. Any derivation here must
explain why a term with two TIME derivatives survives when a term with
none is forbidden, or it fails.

## What must be established (each separately)

- **C1 THE SYMMETRY LEDGER.** State the symmetry that forbids the mass
  term, and determine what it permits. If the field variable is a gauge
  label defined up to a constant shift, compute which local quadratic
  terms survive that shift and which do not. Compute; do not assert.
- **C2 UNIQUENESS AT LEADING ORDER.** Among the terms surviving C1,
  determine whether (mu/2)|da/dt|^2 is the UNIQUE leading-order local
  isotropic term with two time derivatives, or whether alternatives
  exist (e.g. higher time derivatives, anisotropic kernels, nonlocal
  kernels). Alternatives that exist must be named and their exclusion
  argued from registered claims, not from convenience.
- **C3 THE COEFFICIENT.** Separate the FORM from the VALUE. Determine
  whether mu's identification with the rope mass density is derived,
  or whether it is a normalization -- and say so plainly. A derivation
  of the form with an undetermined coefficient is a PARTIAL result and
  must be registered as one.

## Verdict grammar (pre-committed)

- **FORM-FORCED**: C1 and C2 succeed. The kinetic term's form is
  derived; blocker (ii) discharges to whatever extent C3 allows, and
  C3's outcome is stated separately.
- **FORM-UNDERDETERMINED**: alternatives survive C2. The assumption
  stands and the surviving alternatives are named.
- **CONTRADICTION**: the no-material-velocity theorems forbid the term
  entirely, in which case EM-010 has a deeper problem than an
  assumption and that is registered at full volume.
