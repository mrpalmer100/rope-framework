# FND-024 — What carries the nonlocal conditional? Locked bars

## Commission
FND-023 named the sharper question: whether the volume multiplier is the right
CARRIER for the nonlocal conditional, since QB-007 needs spacelike depletion of a
wave amplitude and a pressure-like multiplier is not obviously what depletes one.

## Locked bars
B1 READ WHAT IS ACTUALLY REQUIRED, from QB-007 and QB-009 rather than from the
   phrase "spacelike depletion". State the mechanism those claims demonstrate.
B2 IS THE VOLUME MULTIPLIER THE RIGHT CARRIER? Test it against QB-007's own
   no-go on continuous classical fields.
B3 IF NOT, identify the carrier the corpus's own claims point at, and check it
   against four requirements: global reach, exactness, non-wave character
   (for FND-023's Bancal exemption), and whether it is measured rather than
   postulated.
B4 WHY THE DISTINCTION MATTERS QUANTITATIVELY -- point at a number in the corpus
   that separates the two candidates.
B5 WHAT REMAINS OPEN. This must not be reported as the measurement problem
   solved.
