# GRV-056 — Can strand mechanics produce the shift? Locked bars

## Commission
GRV-055 found the one-metric bijection extends under time dependence but only
onto ZERO-SHIFT metrics, the three missing functions being exactly the shift
vector, and left the verdict deliberately open: a DEFECT if strand mechanics can
supply the missing d_t d_a cross term, a PREDICTION if it cannot.

## Locked bars
B1 IDENTIFY WHAT PRODUCES THE CROSS TERM. Determine which quadratic-derivative
   Lagrangian structure yields a d_t d_a term in the Euler-Lagrange operator,
   and report whether it is unique.
B2 IDENTIFY IT PHYSICALLY. If a structure is found, determine what it
   corresponds to in the medium — expand the kinetic term under whatever
   substitution generates it.
B3 CHECK IT AGAINST THE COPRUS. Does any registered claim permit or forbid that
   structure? The answer must be read from the registry, not assumed.
B4 THE ALTERNATIVE ROUTE, checked and not skipped: the corpus's strands are
   FRAMED and carry twist (FND-STRAND-002/003). Determine whether twist-bend
   coupling supplies a cross term, since a gyroscopic mechanism is the obvious
   candidate a reader would ask about.
B5 THE VERDICT: defect or prediction. If prediction, state what it predicts and
   how it could be tested. If defect, state what would have to be added.
