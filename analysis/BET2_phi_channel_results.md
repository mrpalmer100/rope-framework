curl-free and divergence-free subspaces intersect only at zero, so a

Shown symbolically rather than by appeal to the word "transverse", as
the locked bar required.

charge (GG-006, EM-001). Identifying it with phi would make the source
its own mediator.

**L is the only survivor -- and it has the right property for an
independent reason.** B2: the static Green's function of (-lap + m^2) is
exp(-mr)/4 pi r, which reduces to the Coulomb 1/4 pi r only in the
gapless limit. Long-range 1/r therefore REQUIRES an exactly gapless
mediating channel. EM-RECON-012 (Derived) establishes that a mass term
for this sector is FORBIDDEN -- u is gauge, there are no material
points, so no m^2 u^2 term can be written. **The gaplessness is derived
in principle, not tuned**, and it is precisely what Coulomb demands.
The registered photon-mass bound (~1e-18 eV) is satisfied trivially by
an exactly massless channel.

**VERDICT: CHANNEL-FORCED.** The identification is not a modelling
choice: over the closed inventory, phi can ride nothing else. Blocker
