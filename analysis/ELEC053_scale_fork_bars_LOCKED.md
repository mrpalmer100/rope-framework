tension). Lattice energy density is proportional to E^2. THEREFORE nu tracks
E^2, and the equivalent uniform radius at fixed strand count is the
E^2-weighted one -- ELEC-052's R_eq, not the E-weighted RMS, not the Clem
length, not the intrinsic tail.
