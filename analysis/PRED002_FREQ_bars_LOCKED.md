## The physics, fixed before computing

R1 TOPOLOGICAL / CHERN-SIMONS: rotation from a pseudoscalar coupling, angle
   = (g/2) x field excursion, INDEPENDENT of frequency -> n = 0.
R2 MATERIAL OPTICAL ACTIVITY: a medium with a structural chirality length a

   (This is the standard Drude optical-rotatory-dispersion result, psi ~ 1/lambda^2.)
For comparison, Faraday rotation gives n = -2.
