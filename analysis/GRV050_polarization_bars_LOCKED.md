# GRV-050 — Does a ratchet event radiate in the metric channel? Locked bars

## Commission
GRV-049 demoted the whisper to T2 because neither branch of its channel fork is
checkable, and named the hinge: does a strand-medium excitation couple as a
metric perturbation? GRV-040's own docstring already lists this as an open
refinement ("which sector carries the escaped quantum to infinity -- photon vs
gravitational channel"). The 1+1 chain that produced the whisper CANNOT answer
it: a one-dimensional model carries no polarization content.

## What decides it, fixed before computing
In an elastic medium a local disturbance radiates two families:
 - the TRACE (isotropic/volumetric) part -> longitudinal, scalar, spin-0;
 - the DEVIATORIC (shear) part -> transverse, spin-2 content.
LIGO responds ONLY to transverse-traceless spin-2 strain. The corpus's own
registered longitudinal channel is DECOUPLED FROM MATTER (predictions paper P8),
so a scalar-dominated event is invisible to any matter-based detector.
THEREFORE: decompose the stress change of a reconnection event into trace and
deviatoric parts. The deviatoric fraction is the answer.

## The model, fixed before running
A strand of tension T along unit tangent n contributes stress sigma_ij =
T n_i n_j per unit length. At an X-crossing two strands with tangents n1, n2
(opening half-angle theta) reconnect: the through-pairing is replaced by the
cross-pairing, whose tangents are the bisectors m1 ~ (n1+n2), m2 ~ (n1-n2).
Delta_sigma = T(m1 m1^T + m2 m2^T - n1 n1^T - n2 n2^T), LENGTH-NORMALISED so
that the comparison is at fixed total strand length (the reconnection conserves
strand material).

## Locked bars
B1 TRACE TEST: compute Tr(Delta_sigma) at fixed total length. If it vanishes
   identically, the event has NO volumetric part and radiates purely
   deviatorically.
B2 DEVIATORIC FRACTION across the full crossing-angle range, with the
   degenerate cases (theta -> 0, pi/2) reported rather than excluded.
B3 THE SPIN CONTENT: project the deviatoric part onto the transverse-traceless
   basis for propagation along a general direction and report the mean TT
   fraction over solid angle. A nonzero mean TT fraction is what LIGO couples to.
B4 THE VERDICT, stated either way, INCLUDING the outcome that would close
   GRV-040 permanently (scalar-dominated -> invisible to matter detectors).
B5 HONESTY: this is a STRESS-SOURCE argument, not a propagation calculation.
   It shows what the event sources, not that the medium's tensor mode carries
   it to infinity with GRV-025's Einstein-Hilbert structure. State the gap.
