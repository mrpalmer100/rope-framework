# GRV-072 — K_0, the one computable coefficient: locked bars

## Commission
GRV-071 found four of five gravitomagnetic coefficients absent from the
registered action, and named K_0 -- the couple-stress modulus -- as the one
computable in principle from FND-STRAND-002's existing rod coupling. This
attempts it.

## Locked bars
B1 READ THE MODEL, not a description of it: state the actual energy functional
   in benchmarks/foundations/strand_twist_transport.py and what its parameters
   mean.
B2 TAKE THE CONTINUUM LIMIT and identify gamma.
B3 DIMENSIONAL CHECK: in GRV-068's operator the term is (gamma/2)|k phi|^2 with
   phi dimensionless. Determine gamma's units and whether they are consistent.
B4 CONVERT TO PHYSICAL UNITS -- or establish that the conversion needs an input
   the corpus lacks, and name it. A dimensional estimate may be offered ONLY
   with its assumption stated as an assumption.
B5 STATE THE OUTCOME: is K_0 now derived, estimated, or still absent? Do not
   record an estimate as a derivation.
