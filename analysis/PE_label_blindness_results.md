# COMMISSION PE -- RESULTS: THE AUDIT IS NOT CHEAP; ONE DISTURBED CLASS AND A NAMED SECOND GRANT

*Adjudicated 2026-08-11 after bar lock
(analysis/PE_label_blindness_bars_LOCKED.md). Benchmark:
benchmarks/foundations/pe_label_blindness.py. Sweep over 587 claims. No
grant adopted, widened, or narrowed here.*

## The prior the bar refused, and was right to refuse

FND-053 priced the audit as expected-cheap on the FND-050 argument: the
derivations never mention labels. The locked bar recorded that argument as
INSUFFICIENT before the sweep, on the ground that a derivation need not
mention a degree of freedom to depend on HOW MANY THERE ARE. The sweep
confirms the bar, not the prior.

## The sweep (mechanical net, then adjudication)

Flagged by risk class (claims may fall in several): R-STATE 36, R-MODE 54,
R-COUNT 55, R-STAT 21. The great majority adjudicate SAFE-BY-ARGUMENT for
one of two reasons -- the counted object is a geometric or topological
quantity that labels do not multiply (coverage thresholds, linking
numbers, ropelength, curvature), or the count is of TUBES/ROPES rather
than of internal strand states. Those are recorded in the results table
and are not the finding.

## THE DISTURBED CLASS (the finding)

**The grant makes labels DYNAMICAL.** GRANT-N2 as adopted grants label
EXCHANGE with an energy -- that is a coupling, not a passive tag. A
dynamical internal index of multiplicity N multiplies every count over
strand states. Four registered counts carry a strand index:

1. **PHOTON POLARIZATIONS -- DISTURBED, and decisively.** EM-RECON-022/023
   killed the screw/torsion light candidate ON STATE COUNT (one
   polarization against the photon's two; kill class 4). Turn that same
   instrument, symmetrically, on the surviving carrier: the collective
   transverse Goldstone pair (EM-RECON-025) runs on labeled strands, so
   its state count is 2N -- six at N = 3 -- against a measured two. **By
   the corpus's own kill criterion, applied to itself, GRANT-N2 kills the
   registered light carrier** unless label excitations are gapped out of
   the photon's spectrum.
2. **Vacuum zero-point mode sum (GRV-004, FND-MATTER-014) -- DISTURBED in
   normalization:** the fence and Sigma_vac scale linearly with mode
   multiplicity. Every vacuum-facing number would move by N.
3. **Horizon entropy (GRV-034..039) -- DISTURBED in coefficient:** the
   microstate count gains (A/a^2) ln N, shifting the area law's
   coefficient.
4. **Tube strand count n = 152-156 (ELEC-052/081, FND-030) -- OPEN:**
   whether the lattice-measured profile counts strands or LABELED strands
   decides whether Sigma_eff rescales by N. This is a definitional
   question the grant did not settle.

Items 2-4 are conditional on item 1's resolution and are not separately
prosecuted here.

## The escape, named and unpaid

All four disturbances vanish if the label sector is **GAPPED**:
exchangeable between tubes (the grant's purpose, a virtual/off-shell
process) but not excitable as propagating modes at accessible energies
(the photon count's requirement). This is exactly the structure gauge
theory has -- colour is not excitable in asymptotic states -- so the
escape is physically standard. It is also, in this corpus, ONTOLOGICALLY
UNPAID: the gap is a new scale, not registered, not derived, and not
supplied by GRANT-N2 as adopted.

## NAMED FOR THE AUTHOR (not adopted, per the standing rule)

**GRANT-N2-GAP:** the label sector carries a confinement-like gap placing
label excitations above all accessible energies, so labels are
exchangeable but not radiatable.

- **What it buys:** all four disturbances close at once; EM-RECON-025
  survives with its state count intact; the vacuum and entropy
  normalizations revert.
- **What it costs:** a SECOND primitive stacked on the first, and a new
  unregistered scale. GRANT-N2's price, quoted to the author at adoption
  as one primitive plus one coupling, is now honestly **two primitives,
  one coupling, and one new scale** -- the re-statement the locked bar
  requires.
- **The derivation demand attached:** the gap should not stay granted. The
  corpus's own confinement machinery (the tube sector) is the natural
  place to derive it, and a derivation would retire GRANT-N2-GAP to a
  theorem. That is the sector's named next-order either way.

## Verdict

The audit does NOT pass cheap. One class DISTURBED (decisively, by the
corpus's own kill criterion), two DISTURBED in normalization, one OPEN.
The grant remains as adopted -- this commission may not narrow it -- but
its price is re-stated, and the author now has a second decision on the
same primitive. FND-053's "expected cheap" is registered as a PRIOR THAT
FAILED, which is the audit having done its job.
