# COMMISSION RESH -- DOES THE COLLECTIVE MODE INHERIT A MATERIAL CUTOFF? (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before any number is evaluated. FND-058 closed the
loaded-continuum escape on a step it stated so it could be attacked:
"the band ceiling follows the carriers." That is true for a LATTICE mode.
This commission gives the escape its best case and asks whether a
COLLECTIVE mode on the registered mesh evades it.*

## The escape's best case (constructed FOR it, before judging it)

The registered ontology's strands are CONTINUOUS along their own length
(no material points, FND-REL-002; no registered substructure). Discreteness
is TRANSVERSE only -- crossings at spacing a (EM-RECON-025's stiffness
matrix). Therefore:

- along a strand's own direction the displacement field is a continuum
  and admits arbitrarily large wavenumber -- NO Brillouin cutoff;
- across the mesh, coherence must be sampled at the crossing spacing a,
  so those wavevector components are limited to |k_perp| <= pi/a.

This is the strongest form of the escape available in registered
structure, and it is granted in full for the purposes of the test.

## The test (pre-committed)

If the accessible wavevector region is a SLAB (unbounded along strand
axes, bounded transverse) rather than a sphere, then high-energy photons
can propagate only within a narrow CONE about each strand axis. Compute
that cone's half-angle at the registered anchor energy and confront it
against the observed sky distribution of those photons.

- Anchor energy: 1.4e15 eV (LHAASO Galactic PeV photons; the same anchor
  used by FND-REL-004, FND-057, FND-058).
- Mesh spacing a at BOTH FND-040 floors.
- Half-angle theta_max = arcsin( (pi/a) / |k| ), |k| = E/(hbar c).

## Bars

- **ESCAPE-SUCCEEDS** iff the cone is wide enough to be consistent with
  PeV photons arriving from across the sky -- operationally, iff the
  accessible solid-angle fraction exceeds 10 percent (a deliberately
  generous bar, set before computing).
- **ESCAPE-FAILS-ON-ANISOTROPY** iff the fraction is below that and the
  medium is thereby predicted to be a strong high-energy polarizer of
  direction, contradicting the observed distribution.
- **ESCAPE-SUCCEEDS-ISOTROPICALLY** iff a registered argument removes
  the transverse sampling requirement altogether (which would have to be
  named; the commission must check whether one exists rather than assume
  it does not).

## Mandatory disclosures

  rescue it, and must note that the corpus's OWN isotropy commitments
  (Lorentz invariance in the wave sector, FND-REL-002) are what make the
  anisotropy fatal -- i.e. the framework is killed here by its own
  earlier success, not by an external assumption.
- The commission may not adopt route (c) (registering the sector
  unresolved); it may only recommend it.
