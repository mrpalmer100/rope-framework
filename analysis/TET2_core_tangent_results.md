## T1 -- the tangent field, by matching

The exterior is a winding of GRV-020's internal azimuth with n = 1 about
an axis; ELEC-090 established that strands reach r0 tangent to the core
sphere. A tangent field whose direction tracks the winding phase must
point along the direction in which that phase advances:

    **v = e_phi**, the azimuthal field on S^2.

Its zeros are exactly where |e_phi| = sin(theta) vanishes -- **theta = 0
and theta = pi, the two poles of the winding axis, and nowhere else.**
Near each pole the field is a pure rotation about that point: index +1,
a centre rather than a saddle. Total index 1 + 1 = **2**.

**This independently reproduces ELEC-090's hairy-ball requirement.** The
matching solution was not constructed to satisfy chi(S^2) = 2, and it
does. That is a genuine consistency check between two different
arguments, and it passes.

## T2 -- the alternative is excluded, conditionally and stated as such

Hairy-ball also permits a single index-2 zero. Under axisymmetry -- an
assumption inherited from the ELEC-073/074 profile construction, not
introduced here -- every tangent field on S^2 must vanish at BOTH poles,
since the tangent plane at a pole admits no rotation-invariant nonzero
vector. **The single-zero option is therefore excluded, and the two-pole
structure is forced.**

Without axisymmetry it would survive. The exclusion is conditional on an
assumption the corpus already carries, and the conditionality is stated
rather than buried.

## VERDICT (structure): FIELD-DETERMINED

**The electron's core is a sphere of radius r0 on which the strands run
azimuthally about the winding axis, with exactly two distinguished
points -- the poles -- where that circulation vanishes.**

The corpus can now say what the core looks like, which it could not this
morning.

## T3 -- the angular-momentum confrontation, run because the bar required it

The matched field circulates. Under n -> -n the phase advance reverses,
so the circulation reverses with it. **If that circulation were the
spin, charge would fix spin direction** -- and electrons of one charge
demonstrably occur with both spin orientations. That would have been a
falsification, and the bar forbade avoiding the question.

**It does not hold, for a registered reason.** GRV-020's broken symmetry
acts on the INTERNAL azimuth; the orientation of the winding AXIS in
laboratory space is a separate and unconstrained degree of freedom. n
fixes the circulation sense *relative to its own axis*, not where that
axis points. Reversing the axis in space reverses the observed
circulation at fixed n.

**VERDICT T3: SPIN-INDEPENDENT.** Charge fixes the helicity relation
between winding and circulation; it does not fix spin in the laboratory.

## The result that falls out, and its honest status

The two spin states are naturally read as **the two orientations of the
core axis** -- and the axis exists because the hairy-ball theorem forces
distinguished points, which is a derived fact rather than an assumption.
A two-state internal orientation is the right shape for spin-1/2, and it
arrives from topology rather than from being put in.

**This is suggestive, not derived, and is registered as such.** Nothing
here computes a magnetic moment, a gyromagnetic ratio, or a 4pi rotation
periodicity, and without at least one of those the shape-match is an
encouraging coincidence rather than a result. Naming it as more would be
exactly the overreach this corpus exists to avoid.

## NEW DEBT, created by this commission and registered rather than left implicit

The T3 resolution requires the core's axis to be **free** -- not
energetically pinned to the surrounding weave. The corpus does not
currently have an account of why. If the ambient mesh (with its three
strand families and registered spacing) preferentially orients the core
axis, the two spin states would not be degenerate, and the resolution
above weakens.

**Named target: show that the core axis is unpinned in the registered
weave, or compute the pinning energy and confront it against the
observed degeneracy of the two spin states.** This is a falsifiable
demand with an obvious experimental partner, and it is the price of the
answer this commission gave.
