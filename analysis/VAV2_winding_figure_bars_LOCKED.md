# COMMISSION VAV-2 -- THE WINDING FIGURE VERIFIED (BARS, LOCKED)

*Locked 2026-08-11, before checking any panel. An author-supplied figure
(figures/electron_winding.png) states a strong structural claim about what
"winding" means for the electron. A figure that enters the corpus is a
claim like any other and is verified panel by panel against the registry
BEFORE installation is endorsed. The figure may be found WRONG, PARTLY
WRONG, or CORRECT-AND-SHARPER-THAN-THE-TEXT; all three are reportable.*

## The figure's assertions, itemized before checking

- A1 A rope is two strands helically twisted about a centreline, and this
  twist exists everywhere, including in empty space.
- A2 The electron is a winding of the TWIST/ORIENTATION field, not of the
  ropes: ropes pass straight through the region.
- A3 Walking once around the core, the twist orientation turns through
  2 pi -- winding number n = 1; the positron is n = -1.
- A4 The rope centrelines are NOT deformed around the electron ("not a
  physical loop"), only their twist orientation varies with position.
- A5 The object is a vortex in a field, analogous to a whirlpool: the
  constituents do not orbit; the field circulates.
- A6 The winding pattern can ROTATE in time while the ropes still pass
  straight through (barber-pole dynamics).

## Verification rule (pre-committed)

Each assertion is graded against registered claims by NAME:
- **SUPPORTED-DERIVED** (a Derived claim states it),
- **SUPPORTED-MODELED**,
- **UNREGISTERED** (not contradicted, but no claim states it),
- **IN TENSION** (a registered claim says otherwise).

Any IN TENSION finding must be reported at full volume and must NOT be
resolved by preferring the figure -- if the corpus contains two pictures
of the electron, that is a finding about the corpus, and the adjudication
between them is a separate commission, not a caption edit.

## Installation rule

The figure is installed with a caption that states its verification
grade honestly, including any tension. A figure that overstates its
registered support is worse than no figure.
