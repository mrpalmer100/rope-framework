# ELEC-060 — Pricing the two-medium option (thick/thin instantiation): locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-057 left a fork: abandon the one-medium declaration (ELEC-038) or retire
the hbar sector. ELEC-058 and ELEC-059 closed the two escapes that would have
avoided it (bundling; rope width). The abandonment branch is now the only
unexplored one, and it has a concrete instantiation: TWO STRAND SPECIES, a
thick one near the Lorentz bound and a thin one near 5e-21, so that the hbar
and electron sectors each get the scale they need.

## What must be priced (fixed before computing)
P1 DOES IT ACTUALLY RESCUE THE HBAR SECTOR? ELEC-057's G4 failure was against
   the LORENTZ BOUND (a >= 6.75e-16 needed, bound is 1e-16), NOT against the
   electron sector. The Lorentz bound constrains VACUUM structure. Determine
   whether splitting the medium relieves it. If it does not, two media rescue
   the electron only and the hbar sector stays dead -- which must be stated as
   the headline, not buried.
P2 THE FLUX-TUBE QUESTION, the branch point: T0 is derived as T_tube/n, tying
   the vacuum strand tension to the MEASURED hadronic tube. In a two-medium
   world the tube is made of ONE species. Price both sub-branches:
   (a) tube = vacuum strands: the hbar chain keeps its measured anchor and
       keeps ELEC-057's exclusion with it;
   (b) tube = matter ropes: the vacuum tension is FREED from measurement --
       compute what T0 would then have to be for the hbar sector to work, and
       state plainly whether that is a derivation or a fit.
P3 THE RATIO. The two species differ by 5.2 decades. Ask whether anything in
   the corpus supplies that number. If nothing does, it is a second free
   parameter, and the count of unexplained numbers must be reported honestly.
P4 THE COUPLING CONSTRAINT. ELEC-040's tension matching (an electron rope must
   be a whole number of vacuum strands) was forced BY one medium. State what
   replaces it, and whether the two species can interact at all -- if they
   cannot, the unification that motivated the framework is gone regardless.
P5 THE VERDICT FORMAT. Report what SURVIVES, what is LOST, and the net change
   in free parameters. No branch may be declared preferable on grounds of
   parsimony alone, since parsimony is exactly what the split spends.
