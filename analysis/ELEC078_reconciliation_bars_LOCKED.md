# ELEC-078 — Reconciling EM-RECON-011's two legs. Locked bars

## Commission
ELEC-077 registered an internal strain in EM-RECON-011: leg (1) asserts
longitudinal displacement u is GAUGE because strands have no material points,
while leg (2) computes a cubic vertex from an ELASTIC ENERGY in u. It named the
reconciliation as the next-order and made every matter-to-longitudinal-sector
claim conditional until it is done.

## The reconciliation to be tested
u itself may be gauge while u' is NOT: a medium of strands has a strand DENSITY
n(x), and compression gives u' = -delta_n/n, which is observable without any
material points. If the energy depends on u only through u', both legs can
stand.

## What that then forces, and it must be followed through
Strand number is CONSERVED, so integral u' dx = 0 is a GLOBAL constraint on the
physical configuration. Free pointwise minimisation over u' -- the procedure
ELEC-068 used -- ignores it. The constrained problem must be solved with a
Lagrange multiplier and the resulting effective energy compared with BOTH
previously used functionals.

## Locked bars
B1 Confirm the energy depends on u only through u'.
B2 State the density reading and whether it reconciles the legs.
B3 SOLVE THE CONSTRAINED PROBLEM and report the effective energy. Compare its
   quartic coefficient with the free-minimisation and pure-tension values.
B4 THE CONSEQUENCE for a LOCALISED object in an infinite medium, computed not
   asserted. If the attraction vanishes, say so plainly -- it would mean a THIRD
   model, and that all soliton work in this line rests on the wrong one.
B5 HONESTY: this is a candidate reconciliation, not the only possible one, and
   the corpus should not treat it as settled by one session.
