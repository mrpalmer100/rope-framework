# PRED-002 confronted — locked bars

## Commission
PRED-002 predicts EB/EE = sin(4 beta)/2, FLAT in multipole, and registered the
flatness as "the rope-specific signature" that discriminates against axion-like
and early-dark-energy models. The test was assumed to await LiteBIRD. It does
not: Ballardini, Gruppuso, Paradiso, Sirletti & Natoli, JCAP 09 (2025) 075
[arXiv:2507.16714] tested the scale dependence of beta directly on Planck
legacy data.

## Data logged before computing
S1 Ballardini et al. 2025: beta = 0.30 +/- 0.05 deg (68% CL, Planck legacy,
   excluding instrumental-polarisation-angle systematics). TWO independent
   analyses of scale dependence: a power-law fit to beta_ell, and a
   non-parametric Bayesian reconstruction.
   RESULT: no significant scale dependence (up to 1.8 sigma); the CONSTANT
   model is FAVOURED BY BAYESIAN EVIDENCE; robust across all four published
   Planck CMB solutions.
S2 Eskilt & Komatsu 2022: beta = 0.342 +/- 0.094 deg — the value PRED-002 used.

## Locked bars
B1 THE SHAPE TEST. Does the published scale-dependence analysis confirm or
   refute PRED-002's flatness prediction? State it plainly.
B2 THE NUMBER, recomputed at S1's beta as well as S2's, with both bands.
B3 THE DISCRIMINATION AUDIT, and this is the bar most likely to flatter and
   must not: does flatness actually distinguish the rope from the leading
   alternative? Read S1's own framing — it says scale dependence arises IF the
   ultra-light (pseudo)scalar hypothesis does NOT hold, which implies the
   standard axion ALSO predicts constant beta. If so, PRED-002's registered
   claim that flatness is "the rope-specific signature" OVERSTATES its
   discriminating power and must be corrected on the record.
B4 THE VERDICT: confirmed / refuted / confirmed-but-non-discriminating.
B5 WHAT WOULD DISCRIMINATE, if flatness does not.
