# ELEC-066 — The dynamical-electron conjecture: a scope note, not a result

Filed 2026-08-01. **This is a registered CONJECTURE with no computation behind
it.** It is recorded because it identifies a premise the sector's strongest
closure silently rests on, and because that premise has never been examined.

## The premise nobody named

Every electron claim in the corpus treats the electron as a STATIC GEOMETRY: a
fixed configuration whose properties are read off its shape. ELEC-041's
clasp-and-loop is a stable form with fixed ratios (asymmetry 18.3:1, planarity
275:1, the isoperimetric identity), and ELEC-036 tests it through the rms charge
radius — a static-configuration observable computed from the model's own Poisson
source.

That premise is load-bearing in a place nobody has flagged. Because the clasp is
rigid, its size scales LINEARLY with the strand spacing, r_e ∝ a. **ELEC-057's
5.2-decade no-go used exactly that linearity** to sweep the electron gate across
the whole allowed range of the free length. The sweep is correct as computed.
Its SCOPE is narrower than it reads: it forecloses a static electron at every
strand scale. It does not obviously foreclose an electron whose extent is set by
something other than rigid rescaling.

## Three things pointing the same way

1. **The failing observable is a static one.** ELEC-036's four-order miss is on
   the charge radius of a fixed configuration.
2. **The rigidity is what makes the no-go sweepable.** r_e ∝ a is a consequence
   of staticness, not an independent fact.
3. **A static clasp has nowhere to put mass.** PM-005 registers mass as an input
   with structure unbuilt. An object with internal motion has energy in that
   motion, which is at least the right KIND of thing for mass to be.

## The wall any dynamical proposal must clear

ELEC-043 killed the collective-number escape on causality: transverse waves in
this medium travel at exactly c, so during an event of duration tau only strands
within c tau participate. **A dynamical electron inherits that constraint
undiminished.** Whatever it does internally, it does at the one speed the medium
permits. This is the same wall the gauge branch hit (ROPE-SOURCE-AUDIT-002) and
the same one that closed the bundle escape (ELEC-058): the framework's constraint
structure is tight, and it is tight specifically against proposals needing
coordination faster than the medium supports.

So the surviving shape is narrow: **dynamical WITHOUT long-range coordination** —
a local circulation or self-sustaining excitation whose extent is set by the
medium itself rather than by how far a signal can reach in the event's duration.

## The parallel worth noticing

That is the same shape as the open ħ problem. ELEC-054 found the standing-wave
amplitude is mesoscopic while every length the medium owns is microscopic, and no
candidate bridged sixty spacings — the sector's one live question being what
SELECTS a scale. The electron sector, approached this way, asks for the same
missing thing: an object that picks its own extent.

**Two sectors, stuck on one absence.** That may be a coincidence of framing. It
may also be the most useful structural observation available about where this
programme is blocked.

## What would make this a claim rather than a note

- A dynamical electron model with a stated internal motion, from which a charge
  radius can be computed and confronted with ELEC-036's bound.
- A demonstration that its extent is NOT rigidly proportional to the strand
  spacing — which is what would put it outside ELEC-057's sweep rather than
  inside it.
- Compliance with ELEC-043's causal budget, computed rather than assumed.

Until those exist this is an intuition, and it is filed as one. It should not be
cited as support for anything.

## AMENDMENT 1 (2026-08-01, same day) — the causal wall is TRANSVERSE-ONLY

The section above ("The wall any dynamical proposal must clear") is **too
strong, and was written having forgotten a channel the corpus already
registers.**

ELEC-043's causal budget bounds coordination by c·tau because **transverse**
waves in this medium travel at exactly c. But the medium has a second sector.
EM-RECON-011 registers the longitudinal sector as the gauge-content/tension
channel: gapless, stiffened (c_L_eff > c_L), decoupling from matter and light at
linear order, and creating no causal paradox because the framework carries a
preferred frame — "a real, dark, falsifiable fast channel, not a contradiction".
EM-RECON-012 (*Derived*) removes the twist-stretch gap and confirms the sector is
gapless in principle. QB-008 then corners the speed from experiment:
anticorrelation geometry demands v > ~10c, Bell timing demands v > 1.38e4 c, and
Bancal's signalling theorem excludes ALL finite speeds — forcing the channel onto
the **instantaneous-constraint limb, which is exactly the ideal limit of the
corpus's own P-VOL inextensibility postulate.**

So: **an inextensible medium coordinates along its tension channel instantly.**
The wall I described applies to transverse coordination and not to constraint
propagation. A dynamical electron whose internal coherence is maintained through
the tension/constraint channel is not bounded by c·tau at all, and the "narrow
surviving shape" claimed above — dynamical without long-range coordination — was
narrowed by an oversight rather than by the physics.

### What this does NOT dissolve

One counter-constraint is registered in the same place and must be carried:
**the longitudinal sector decouples from matter and light at linear order,
exactly** (EM-RECON-011). A dynamical electron is matter. If its internal
coordination rides the tension channel, that coupling has to be nonlinear, or
the decoupling theorem has to be shown not to apply to a self-coupled
configuration. Neither has been done. That is now the sharp question, and it is
a much better question than the one this note originally posed.

### The parallel strengthens rather than weakens

QB-007 isolated the irreducible core of the measurement problem — winner-take-all
localization requires spacelike depletion — and named the preferred-frame fast
channel as **the only native candidate**. The pilot-wave branch
(QGATE-011..017) is de Broglie–Bohm-adjacent and needs exactly an instantaneous
constraint (QB-010). So the same channel is the standing candidate for the
electron's internal coherence, for measurement, and plausibly for ELEC-054's
scale selection. **Three sectors, one channel, and it is already registered as
Derived-in-structure and cornered by experiment onto the instantaneous limb.**

That is a considerably more interesting position than this note reported before
the amendment, and the amendment exists because the author of the note had
forgotten the framework's second sector.
