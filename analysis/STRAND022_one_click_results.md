## The instrument audit (the session's pivot)

The audit table (all points): 240 injected runs, 23 barred-FIREs, but
only ONE genuine nucleation (a persistent cluster at t_first + 25). The
barred FIRE definition — any site's phase momentarily exceeding pi —
measures TRANSIENT OSCILLATION OVERSHOOTS of the strong delocalized
mode, not clicks. At A = 1.0 the injected standing wave swings sites past
pi and back without converting anything: 21 "fires," 20 of them nothing.
The cluster observable, built as B3's alibi, caught its sibling
instrument lying. VERDICT: NO-VERDICT-BY-INSTRUMENT on the exclusivity
bars; nothing about B1's question was measured, and the corpus says so.

## The finding that IS registrable (measured, not barred)

Across 240 runs spanning injected energies from 0.24 to 24 model units —
the top of that range is roughly TWELVE kink-pair costs — a delocalized
coherent excitation at cold temperature produced exactly ONE genuine
nucleation. Meanwhile the same engine at thermal temperature with a
modest tilt nucleates readily (the entire 009..021 record).

CONCENTRATION, NOT ENERGY, IS THE CLICK'S BOTTLENECK. A spread-out
coherent wave, however energetic, does not click; the thermal bath's
role in the registered detector is not merely to pay the Boltzmann
factor but to provide the LOCALIZATION mechanism — rare local
concentrations — that converts delocalized channel energy into a
pointlike event. This sharpens the "particle lives in the detector"
account: the dot requires a concentrator, and in this engine the
concentrator is thermal fluctuation.

It also reframes the exclusivity question more favorably than the
drainage story: if clicks require rare concentration events, one-click
exclusivity may be scarcity of concentrations rather than energy
bookkeeping — a hypothesis for the redesign, not a conclusion.

## Lessons, logged into the standing rules

1. CALIBRATION-BRACKET PRICING: a committed calibration grid must be
   priced against an order-of-magnitude pre-estimate of the onset
   (sibling of the seed/window/tail pricing lessons; the fourth entry).
2. OBSERVABLE PAIRING: a threshold observable (FIRE) must be defined by
   PERSISTENCE, not instantaneous crossing, whenever strong coherent
   drive can overshoot — and pairing it with an independent persistence
   instrument (the cluster count) is what caught this one. The pairing
   becomes the rule; the persistence definition becomes the default.
