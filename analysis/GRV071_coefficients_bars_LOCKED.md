# GRV-071 — Deriving a, b, c, d, K_0: locked bars

## Commission
GRV-070 reduced the gravitomagnetic question to one equation,
(a+b)(c+d)/(4 pi K_0) = -2G/c^3, and named the next task: derive the five
quantities from the strand action, OR demonstrate the action lacks the structure
to determine them. The reviewer stated explicitly that the second outcome is
equally admissible and equally informative. This session attempts the first and
reports whichever it reaches.

## The five quantities
a, b -- how matter's angular momentum couples to the medium's MACROrotation and
        MICROrotation respectively, in L_source = J.(a Omega + b phi)
c, d -- how those fields enter the observable, in g_0i = c Omega_i + d phi_i
K_0  -- the stiffness of the massless collective mode, setting the pole strength

## Locked bars
B1 For EACH of the five, search the corpus and state: DERIVED (with the claim),
   COMPUTABLE IN PRINCIPLE (with the machinery that would do it), or ABSENT.
B2 No quantity may be recorded as derived on the basis of a plausible reading.
   If a claim covers a neighbouring case, say so and say what is missing.
B3 If the action lacks the structure, IDENTIFY EXACTLY WHICH STRUCTURE, so the
   gap is a work item rather than a verdict.
B4 State whether the target equation is currently evaluable.
B5 Report the outcome with the same weight either way, per the reviewer's
   instruction.
