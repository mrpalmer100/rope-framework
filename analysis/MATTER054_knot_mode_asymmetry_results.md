## Bars (locked before computing)

1. **Scope, stated first to prevent a category error:** the registered ΔE_zp
   is a *curvature-conditioning* quantity (κ² per site on a 1D host), NOT a
   count of internal transverse standing waves. The mode band is a different
   object. This session asks only whether it is an OMITTED ADDITIONAL term.
2. Mode counting inherited from MATTER053, not re-chosen: periodic BCs,
   k_n = 2πn/(La), cutoff 1/a, degeneracy 4 per |n|, with the degeneracy
   displayed as a sensitivity since it only scales the result.
3. Magnitude bar, pre-committed: an omitted term is admissible only if it is
   at most comparable to the tension term (factor ≲ 1). More than an order of
   excess = excluded.
4. The discontinuity is part of the same test, not a separate observation.
5. Exclusion is a permitted outcome.

## Verdicts

**Magnitude: excluded by more than three orders.** Adding the internal band
would not correct the mass model; it would replace every knot mass with a
GeV-scale number and destroy the table. The verdict is degeneracy-independent
— stripping all four states still leaves ~3 orders of excess.

**Discontinuity: excluded again, and more sharply.** The ring gets nothing
(empty band) while the trefoil gets a GeV, so an admissible internal term
would introduce a three-order jump between the two lightest knots in the
table. Nothing in the observed mass table does that there. The asymmetry is
therefore not a feature to be modelled but a **reductio** — it is the
sharpest available proof that the internal band cannot be in the mass ledger
at all.

## What this buys (and why it is not an echo of MATTER053)

1. **Independent corroboration.** MATTER053 killed the internal-mode picture

3. **The ambient route is now the only survivor** for the zero-point term,
   reached from three independent directions: the empty band, the excess
   magnitude, and the forbidden discontinuity.
