# ROPE-MODE-003 locked bars — surrounding 3-D field gate

The certified ELEC-009 linked rope is held fixed and embedded in a three-dimensional scalar eigenproblem. The field has a softened central attraction and a tubular rope-coupling potential. No fit to observed atomic spectra is performed.

- **B1 — certified linked rope:** `d_min >= 0.060` and `| |Lk| - 1 | <= 0.03`.
- **B2 — grid convergence:** maximum relative change of the first nine excitation gaps between the 23^3 and 29^3 interior grids is below 8%.
- **B3 — localization:** mean probability inside radius 2.2 for the first twelve modes is at least 85% at every tested rope coupling.
- **B4 — angular families:** the uncoupled central-field baseline contains an identifiable low-lying l=0 mode and at least two l=1 modes, with mean projection purity at least 55% among the first six modes.
- **B5 — robustness:** l=0 and l=1 families remain identifiable at beta=0.25 and 0.5, with mean angular purity at least 45% among the first eight modes.

A positive programme claim requires all five bars. Failure of B3 is interpreted as inadequate finite-volume localization, not as absence of angular mode structure.
