# GRV-055 — Does the one-metric bijection survive time dependence? Locked bars

## Commission
GRV-054 identified DYNAMICS as the gravity sector's gap with data waiting for it
— everything registered is static or quasi-static — and named the route: a
time-dependent metric needs GRV-029's four coefficient functions to become
time-dependent, which is a concrete question about the medium.

## The two questions, kept separate
Q1 FORM. GRV-029 writes the wave operator as mu u_tt = d_a(T_a d_a u). For a
   TIME-DEPENDENT metric, is that the right operator? The metric wave operator
   is (1/sqrt-g) d_mu(sqrt-g g^{mu nu} d_nu u); its time part must be compared.
Q2 COUNTING. GRV-029's bijection rests on four functions matching four. Does the
   count still balance when both sides are allowed time dependence, and when the
   medium's spatial coefficients are allowed to be a full tensor rather than
   diagonal?

## Locked bars
B1 Expand the metric wave operator's time part symbolically and compare with
   mu u_tt. Report the difference exactly.
B2 If they differ, determine which form the MEDIUM's own Lagrangian gives. The
   corpus's medium has L = (mu/2)u_t^2 - (T/2)(grad u)^2; take its
   Euler-Lagrange equation and see which operator it produces. Report whether
   GRV-029 needs correcting or was a special case correctly stated.
B3 THE GENERAL COUNT: allow the medium's spatial coefficients to be a symmetric
   tensor T_ab (which GRV-025's measured shear response requires) and count
   against the metric's independent content. State the result and what it
   restricts.
B4 WHAT THE RESTRICTION FORBIDS, physically and by name. If the medium can only
   realize a subclass of metrics, identify a spacetime it cannot represent.
B5 HONESTY: a counting argument establishes what CAN be represented, not that
   the medium's dynamics produce it. State the gap.
