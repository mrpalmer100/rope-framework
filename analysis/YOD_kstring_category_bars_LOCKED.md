# Commission YOD -- what a k-string IS: locked bars

*Locked 2026-08-11, before adjudication. The question, posed by the
author: are SU(N>=4) k-strings bundles of multiple flux tubes, or
single heavier-charge objects? The answer bears directly on the
FND-047 scope flag: if bundles, the k-string sine-law data confronts a
TUBE-BINDING observable, not the single-source softening prediction,
and the apparent sign contradiction may be a category error.*

## Scope

- Y1 (standard physics, cited): what characterizes the asymptotic
  k-string in SU(N) gauge theory -- representation or N-ality? What
  does gluon screening do to a heavy-representation source at long
  distance? Is sigma_k < k sigma_1 (sub-additivity = binding) in the
  data under BOTH candidate laws?
- Y2 (the corpus's own ontology, registry-cited): in the rope
  framework, what IS a source of N-ality k? Charge is winding
  (GG-006, topologically conserved; the Gauss-law/charge-as-rope-count
  structure). Adjudicate: does the ontology even CONTAIN a
  "single heavier charge" object distinct from k unit windings?
- Y3 (the consequence for the flag): if Y1 and Y2 agree that k-strings
  are bundles, restate what the SU(6) sine-law data does and does not
  confront: (a) the single-source Casimir recruitment/softening
  prediction (PSI/BET/CHET) -- confronted or not? (b) an inter-tube
  binding relation -- registered anywhere? If not, name it as the
  UNCOMPUTED observable the k-string data actually measures, with the
  qualitative sign check performed (does the derived softening predict
  tube ATTRACTION, i.e. sub-additivity, and does the data show it?).
- Y4 (what does NOT move): the SU(3) pin (FND-046/047) is
  single-source intermediate-distance physics and is untouched either
  way; the N-universality scope question remains open but its STAKES
  are re-priced.

## Honesty clauses

1. This commission adjudicates category, not magnitude: no binding
   energy is computed tonight; the bundle-binding relation, if named,
   is named as an open next-order with no number attached.
2. Sub-additivity is checked numerically against both laws at several
   (N, k); the check is a sanity gate, not evidence for the framework.
3. If Y1 and Y2 disagree (standard physics says bundle, ontology says
   otherwise, or vice versa), the disagreement is the finding and is
   registered at full volume.
4. The FND-047 flag is updated to whatever this session finds --
   including if the finding makes the flag WORSE.
