## The grant, stated in full

  GRANT 3 (INDIVISIBLE DELIVERY): a rope excitation carrying one quantum
  of channel energy transfers that energy at absorption as an
  indivisible unit — exactly one nucleation channel receives the full
  quantum; partial delivery does not occur. Which channel receives it is
  governed by the registered Born-from-channel-energy law: the
  probability of channel i is its share of the delivered channel energy,
  P(i) = E_i / sum_j E_j.

## Derivation D1 — beamsplitter statistics (the first consequence)

Setup: one quantum enters a lossless splitter with transmittance
tau = |t|^2, reflectance 1 - tau; arms A and B terminate on threshold
detectors; the wave delivers channel energies E_A = tau E, E_B = (1-tau)E.

(i) SINGLE-ARM STATISTICS: by the grant plus the Born law,
    P(click A) = tau, P(click B) = 1 - tau. At a 50/50 splitter: 50/50 —
    the laboratory fact the classical engine measurably could not
    produce (STRAND-024: 0/64), now derived in one line, with the grant
    supplying exactly the measured gap and nothing more.
(ii) ANTICOINCIDENCE: one indivisible quantum funds one click;
    P(A and B) = 0 exactly, so g2(0) = 0 for single-quantum input —
    the Grangier-Roger result.
(iii) FOCK COUNTING: n independent quanta are delivered independently,
    so (n_A, n_B) ~ Binomial(n, tau) — the quantum beamsplitter counting
    statistics for photon-number states, at zero further assumptions.
(iv) THE CLASSICAL LIMIT, recovered not broken: coherent-class input is
    a Poisson number of quanta, mean lambda; independent Binomial
    thinning gives n_A ~ Poisson(lambda tau) and n_B ~
    Poisson(lambda(1-tau)), INDEPENDENT, hence g2(0) = 1. The grant
    produces antibunching ONLY for sub-Poissonian sources — it cannot
    fake quantumness for classical light, which is itself a
    consistency check most collapse-style postulates fail to state.
(v) THERMAL INPUT: geometric (Bose-Einstein) quantum number thinned
    binomially gives the super-Poissonian g2(0) = 2 both arms and the
    classic thermal cross-correlation — Hanbury Brown-Twiss bunching,
    also for free.

Every statement above is counting arithmetic from the grant plus the
already-registered Born law; the benchmark executes it numerically

## What the grant does NOT buy (stated before anyone asks)

- Hong-Ou-Mandel: the dip is two-quantum EXCHANGE interference; the
  grant is per-quantum and yields the classical 50% visibility cap, not
  the quantum 100%. HOM remains on the far side, as the roadmap says.
- Bell/CHSH, and the entangled quantum eraser: excluded by QB-003,
  unchanged.
- Phases: the grant handles energy bookkeeping; interference of
  amplitudes upstream of absorption is the wave sector's business.

## Falsifiers (the grant is a claim about the world, and can die)

F1: any established observation of PARTIAL-quantum energy deposition at
    a threshold detector (a calorimetric fraction of one quantum
    delivered and the remainder propagating).
F2: heralded-single-photon beamsplitter coincidences inconsistent with
    g2(0) = 0 beyond accidental background.
F3: Fock-input counting statistics deviating from Binomial(n, tau).
F4 (retirement, not falsification): any local dynamics that reproduces
    D1 from below retires the grant into a theorem — the standing offer
    every grant in this corpus carries.
