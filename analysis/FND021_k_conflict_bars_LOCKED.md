# FND-021 — Is the rope inextensible or extensible? Locked bars

## Commission
The operator asked, of the parameter card's statement that T0 is "not stored
elastic energy": if the rope is inextensible, where does the superluminal
longitudinal channel come from? That channel exists BECAUSE the rope stretches.
The question is correct and it exposes two conflicts, one resolvable and one not.

## The claims in tension
FND-017 / P-VOL / FND-STRAND-001: the medium is INEXTENSIBLE ("literal
  inextensible elastic curves"), so T0 is a Lagrange multiplier storing no
  energy, and no local derivation of T0 can succeed.
EM-RECON-009: finite strand EXTENSIBILITY, stretch modulus k, with k > T0
  required for nonlinear stability and k/T0 = 2 fitting nuclear and chemical
  spacings.
EM-RECON-012 (Derived): the longitudinal sector is GAPLESS and its speed is
  STIFFENED -- the fast channel.
QB-008 (EFT-constrained): Bell timing demands v_dep > 1.38e4 c, stated as
  K_L/K_T >= 1.9e8.

## Locked bars

B2 CHECK QB-008's INTERNAL CONSISTENCY: does sqrt(K_L/K_T) reproduce its own
   speed bound?
B3 THE SECOND CONFLICT: EM-RECON-009 uses k/T0 = 2; QB-008 requires k/T0 >= 1.9e8.
   Report the discrepancy and do not choose between them by preference.
