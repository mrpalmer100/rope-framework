## The question

MEM's Chain A conditional: nuclear contact is FULL-SECTION at
constituent scale (contact energy scales linearly in n, every
constituent pair engaged). The registered commentary prices
full-section implausible for touching parallel cylinders. TAV showed
the constituents sit at internal gap 0.799 sigma (f_c) / 0.050 sigma
(hex), on the solid side of the 1.5 sigma crossover. Does the
engine's own contact energetics, at those gaps, return full-section
scaling in any admissible contact geometry -- and if so, at what n
does the effective engaged-pair count m_eff(n) enter the pinned band?

## Instrument, locked

- The FND-STRAND-004 engine contact form: E_pair = Ac/(1 + (r/sigma)^4)
  per closest-approach pair segment, Ac = 1 (engine units),
  sigma = 0.12 (the registered Phase 3 value). Static energetics;
  straight axial strands (the TAV bundle geometry); no dynamics.
- Bundles: circular cross-section, hex-packed and f_c-packed variants,
  n strands each, internal surface-to-surface gap locked at TAV's
  values: g_hex = 0.050 sigma, g_fc = 0.799 sigma. Strand radius
  enters only through the gap convention (centers at pitch
  = 2 r_s + g; r_s = sigma/2 declared here as the contact-range
  radius convention, disclosed as a modeling choice).
- n sweep: hex-centered counts {7, 19, 37, 61, 91} plus {5, 9, 45,
  63, 73, 81} filled to nearest constructible packing, spanning both
  TAV windows [5, 9] and [40, 81] and the MEM pin [63, 73].

## The two geometries, both locked, neither privileged

- G-LAT: two parallel bundles in LATERAL exterior contact,
  surface-to-surface gap equal to the internal gap (the touching
  convention declared here, before any number). This is the picture
  the registered commentary priced.
- G-AX: two coaxial bundles in AXIAL end-proximity, facing cross
  sections at gap equal to the internal gap, pairwise energy summed
  over facing constituent ends (segment-end contact, same form).
  This is the bound-web candidate MEM's note motivated.

## Measurements, locked

- M1: E(n) per unit engaged length (G-LAT) / per facing section
  (G-AX), both packings.
- M2: m_eff(n) = E(n) / E_1, with E_1 the single-pair energy at the
  SAME gap and geometry (self-normalizing; Ac and absolute scale
  cancel).
- M3: fitted scaling exponent p over the sweep, log-log OLS, with
  half-sweep stability check (p on lower half vs upper half within
  0.1, else UNSTABLE and no classification).
