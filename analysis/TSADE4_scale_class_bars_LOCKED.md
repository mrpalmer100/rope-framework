## QUESTION
The corpus's ontology carries THREE hierarchy levels: the ROPE (a
bundle of n_rs vacuum strands, matter sector), the VACUUM STRAND
(spacing a, tension T0), and the SUB-STRAND (FND-087's fine level,
spacing a_f, tension T0_f = T0/n_sub). WHICH LEVEL SETS THE CORPUS'S
ABSOLUTE LENGTHS -- that is, at which level does the one measurement-
fixed calibration (the M-point, FND-MATTER-044: a = 6.0e-17 m,
T0 = 434 J/m) enter, and are the other levels' absolute scales DERIVED
from it, SUSPENDED on underived parameters, or INDEPENDENTLY anchored?

## METHOD, fixed at lock (B1)
Symbol inspection only, per the SHIN8 ruling. Every registered
absolute length in the corpus (the programmatic sweep found 42 claims

docs/NAME_REGISTRY.md (n_rs vs n_sub) and each claim's own text read

commission determines provenance, not values.

## THE THREE ADMISSIBLE VERDICTS, pre-committed (B2)
- SINGLE-ANCHOR: exactly one level carries the measurement-fixed
  calibration and every other registered absolute length is traceable
  to it through registered relations (possibly suspended on m or
  n_sub, which is a named suspension, not a second anchor).
- SPLIT-ANCHOR: two or more levels carry INDEPENDENT absolute
  calibrations with no registered relation between them. This is the
  adverse outcome and registers a consistency debt at full volume:
  two independent length anchors in one ontology must either agree or
  name their tension.
- BLOCKED: the classification cannot be completed by symbol inspection
  because some registered length's level assignment is ambiguous in
  its own text. The ambiguous claims are named; the commission returns
  the list, not a guess.
