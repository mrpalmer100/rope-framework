# FND-023 — Can one medium carry both? Locked bars

## Commission
FND-022 asked whether one elastic medium can carry a finite-speed longitudinal
wave AND an exactly instantaneous constraint, in a preferred frame, without
contradiction. It called that the sharpest open item in foundations.

## Locked bars
B1 RE-EXAMINE FND-022's FRAMING. It asserted the two demands are "opposite
   limits of the same modulus". Check that against the corpus: are P-VOL and the
   strand stretch modulus k the same constraint or different ones?
B2 IS THERE A STANDARD PHYSICAL SYSTEM that carries an instantaneous constraint
   and finite-speed waves simultaneously? If so, name its equations.
B3 BANCAL'S PREMISE. That theorem excludes finite-speed hidden influences by an
   argument that uses relativity of simultaneity. Determine whether the premise
   holds in this framework, citing the relevant claims.
B4 THE VERDICT: is the coexistence coherent, and at what status?
B5 WHAT REMAINS OPEN. Do not report a resolved framing as a completed
   derivation.
