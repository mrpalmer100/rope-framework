## The question

Is Sigma_EM (EM-RECON-014's field-strain calibration constant) derivable
from registered structure -- specifically, is it the same PHYSICAL
QUANTITY as the vacuum tension density the FND-030 series pinned, with
the numerical gap explained rather than explained away?

## The three checks (order fixed)

- **H1 DEFINITIONAL IDENTITY.** Compare the two quantities' DEFINITIONS,
  not their quoted values. Sigma_EM is defined as T0 n_L (vacuum tension
  density). Determine whether the registered mesh supplies n_L. If the
  mesh's line density is registered, compute T0 n_L from it directly.
- **H2 THE PROVENANCE OF THE 1e25.** Determine what kind of number
  EM-RECON-014's ~1e25 J/m^3 IS. If it is a MEASUREMENT, the gap is a
  physical discrepancy and the bridge fails. If it is a BOUND (or a
  bound saturated by assumption), the gap is not a discrepancy at all
  and EM-020's DIFFERENT-OBJECTS verdict requires correction. Read the
  source; do not infer from the quoted value.
- **H3 CONSISTENCY UNDER SUBSTITUTION.** If H1/H2 support the bridge,
  substitute the mesh-derived value into the EM sector's registered
  confrontations -- the nonlinearity onset field against the Schwinger
  scale, and the ATLAS/laser bounds -- and verify nothing breaks.
  Cross-check against FND-031's independently registered E_crit, which
  was computed under the pinned Sigma.
