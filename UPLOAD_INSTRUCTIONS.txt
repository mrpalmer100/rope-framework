--------------------

medium "cannot host" the observed PeV photons. That asserts a proof of

substructure below the measured thickness; a second carrier for the
high-energy quanta) still open and unattempted, and FND-061 having
widened the target from "find an impossibly small length" to "supply
isotropy at high k".

No claim text and no registered result changed. The underlying claims
(FND-058/059/060/061/062) always carried the correct scope on their
faces; the overstatement was only in the summary documents.
