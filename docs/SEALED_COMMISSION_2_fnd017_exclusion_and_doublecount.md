## THE COMMISSION

The repository registers `Sigma = 3 T0 / a^2`, equivalently `T0 = Sigma a^2/3`,
where the factor 3 counts orthogonal strand families threading a cell (this is
established: FND-MATTER-059, benchmark
`benchmarks/foundations/fnd017_three_provenance.py`). One strand's tributary
cross-section is `a^2/3`.

A construction in the matter sector computes the medium content displaced by an
impenetrable inclusion of cross-section `pi r^2`. Two questions, in this order.

**Q1 — THE BOOKKEEPING AUDIT. Answer this first and independently.**

`Sigma = 3 T0 / a^2` already contains the family count. Pricing displaced
strand-equivalents at `a^2/3` per strand reintroduces it.

Determine, from the construction alone: does the displaced-content calculation
reach its answer via `Sigma` (an energy density that already carries the 3), or
via strand counts priced individually (where the 3 has not yet been applied)?

If via `Sigma`, the factor 3 is being counted twice and the correct multiplier
is 1. If via strand counts only, it enters once. State which, cite the lines,
and show the units carrying through. **A double-count is an error wherever it
lands, so this question is answerable without reference to any target and must
be answered without seeking one.**

**Q2 — THE EXCLUSION ARGUMENT. Only if Q1 clears.**

The claim to test: an impenetrable inclusion removes medium content *wholesale
across all three strand families* — it does not selectively evict the family
parallel to it while the two transverse families thread through — and therefore
displaces `pi r^2 / (a^2/3) = 3 pi r^2 / a^2` strand-equivalents.

Can wholesale removal across families be written any other way? Specifically:

- Is there a consistent construction in which an inclusion excludes the
  families anisotropically, or excludes at a density other than the full
  `3/a^2` per unit face area?
- Does the corpus's interpenetrability primitive (FND-MATTER-004: single
  strands interpenetrate freely, tangibility is a density-threshold effect)
  license, forbid, or leave open a per-family exclusion asymmetry?
- Is the parallel to the polarization factor exact? That factor was ruled
  *forced* because the construction cannot be written with one polarization.
  Can this one be written with a partial family count?

**Verdict required:** FORCED (cannot be written otherwise), PERMITTED (a
consistent alternative exists, so the reading is a choice), or UNDETERMINED.
State explicitly whether your answer is a *consequence* of registered relations
or a *reading* of them. A reading is not a derivation and will not be adopted as
one.
