## The finding E established but did not draw
Commission E proved the MATTER055 target converts through T0 exactly
once, and that T0 is the same primitive the m_e calibration spent, its
provenance running through MATTER-044's joint solve. MATTER-044 IS the
M-point. FND-MATTER-049 is REOPENED (the three-pin contradiction,
Commission B). Therefore:

THE LAMBDA TARGET'S ABSOLUTE VALUE IS DOWNSTREAM OF THE DISPUTED
CALIBRATION. The 2.08x near-miss is final WITHIN the registered
calibration; it is NOT final against a scale foundation currently in
dispute.

## The sensitivity, from E's own cube-root note
The scale a enters the residual combination at the third root:
a = (3 * pinned / Sigma)^(1/3). So a hypothetical shift of the
calibrated scale by factor X moves the lambda target by X^(1/3):

  - a moved by 10x   -> target moved by ~2.15x
  - a moved by 1e11  (the full three-pin gap) -> target moved by ~4600x

The first is comparable to the gap itself; the second would obliterate
the near-miss framing entirely. The cube-root cushions but does not
neutralize the dependence.

## What this does and does not do
DOES NOT reopen the mechanism verdict. 060's terminal condition holds:
within the registered calibration the target does not move, no
re-adjudication is triggered, lambda remains Open at 2.08x. The two-seal
campaign's closure stands.

DOES attach a conditionality clause the registry should carry now rather
than rediscover later: a future session that resolves the three-pin
problem (Commissions B-continuation / G / the carrier chain) with a
MOVED M-point owes the lambda campaign a one-line recomputation of the
target, X^(1/3) applied, and a re-read of the 2.08x verdict against the
moved value. This is a named downstream dependency, not an open problem.
