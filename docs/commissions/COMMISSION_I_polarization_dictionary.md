## The question H answered by counting and this commission answers by

H's kill class 4 reasoned: one Goldstone per rope -> one polarization

untraced dictionary assumption: that "polarization state" maps to

The geometric candidate the count ignores: the two-strand rope's
cross-section carries a transverse DIRECTOR (the line joining the strand
centers), and the screw mode modulates that director's angle. The
question is whether observed polarization phenomenology attaches to the
director angle, and if so, whether one angular field can reproduce the
full two-state structure.

## The decidable core
Light's polarization is not merely "two labels"; it is a specific
structure: two orthogonal states supporting INDEPENDENT complex
superposition (relative amplitude AND relative phase), which is what
circular and elliptical polarization, Malus's law at arbitrary angle,
and birefringent walk-off jointly require. Formally: the state space is
(the projectivization of) C^2, carrying a full SU(2)/Poincare-sphere
structure. A single real angular field theta(z,t) on a rope carries, at
fixed wavevector, a different structure, and whether the difference is
observable through the registered dictionary is the trace.

## THE PHYSICAL STATEMENT OF KILL CLASS 4 (Mark, 2026-08-05; the trace
## must confront both objections by name)
The guitar-string argument. A string vibrates in x OR in y: two
independent transverse directions. That two-dimensionality is what
linear polarization at arbitrary angle IS, and circular polarization is
nothing separate -- it is x and y a quarter-cycle apart. Polarized
glasses work by killing one axis and passing the other. Against this:

  OBJECTION 1 (one azimuthal direction): a pure twist wave swings each
  strand AZIMUTHALLY, in the ONE direction the rope's local geometry
  dictates. Flipping the twist deviation's sign gives the same
  oscillation a half-cycle later, not a second independent state (a
  swing pushed backward is not a different swing). A scalar angle
  field has no second dimension to phase against, so naive twist
  cannot build linear polarization at arbitrary angle, and the
  sign-of-twist helicity candidate (proposed in conversation,
  retracted on this objection) fails: helicity needs two dimensions
  with a phase relationship.

  OBJECTION 2 (the corkscrew): the rope's standing pitch tau0 rotates
  the director along its length at sub-nuclear scale, so over one
  optical wavelength the local swing plane corkscrews an enormous
  number of times. A fixed-angle polarizer would then see the same
  pitch-averaged transmission at EVERY orientation, and polarized
  glasses observably do not behave that way. Any surviving mapping
  must either decouple the observable E-direction from the
  instantaneous swing direction or show why the pitch average does
  not wash out Malus.

Both objections are physical restatements of the count kill, sharper
than the count because they say WHICH experiments fail and HOW. The
trace's verdicts on (a)-(c) must address each objection explicitly: a
mapping survives only if it answers both. "The dictionary is silent on
the E-direction" is a live possibility (the EM-RECON-014 bridge was
identified through ENERGY, never through direction) and is recorded as
SILENT per I5, with the direction mapping named as the minimal missing
commitment.
