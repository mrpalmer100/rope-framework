# COMMISSION N — THE DIRECTION OF THE ELECTRIC FIELD
## Charter (2026-08-09). Bars locked at chartering, before any computation.

## The question
EM-RECON-019 (Open) found by trace that the registered EM sector commits E's
MAGNITUDE (EM-RECON-014 bridge), E's static/induction SPLIT, and E's curl
partner B — but the transverse DIRECTION of E at the mechanical-carrier level
is committed in NO registered claim. The only directional language in the
corpus is unregistered guide prose. Commission N asks: **can the direction of
E be DERIVED from registered commitments, and if not, what is the minimal
additional commitment, named honestly?**

## Registered inputs (the ONLY allowed foundations)
- **The operational definition, already in registered use**: E is force per
  winding (F = qE with q = winding number, GRV-020). Precedent: EM-015's
  electrostatic sign derivation computes the STATIC direction exactly this way
  (gradient of the constraint interaction energy → radial). No new adoption.
- EM-RECON-014: the energy bridge g = E·sqrt(eps0/SIGMA) (magnitude only).
- The registered contact form: V depends on CENTER-LINE separation only
  (azimuth-blind; reproduced in EM-RECON-023).
- EM-RECON-011/021: transverse ψ decouples from longitudinal at linear order;
  ψ = 0 is exact (all orders).
- The channel-map (EM-RECON-022): the registered facts about which mechanical
  dofs exist — transverse displacement (two components), twist (one), stretch.
- Phenomenological constraints ALREADY registered as identities: Malus
  cos²θ demands a genuine transverse VECTOR (the pitch-average-1/2 kill for
  single-field candidates, EM-RECON-019's trace); the static direction
  (radial) from EM-015.

## Pre-committed bars (LOCKED)
- **N1 (provenance)**: every step uses only the inputs above; any additional
  assumption is labeled ADOPTED at the moment it enters, or the run stops.
- **N2 (definition first)**: the operational definition of E-direction (force
  direction on a test winding) is fixed BEFORE any candidate is computed, and
  it must be the SAME definition that already governs the static sector — one
  definition, both regimes, or the split is registered as a failure.
- **N3 (double confrontation)**: any derived direction must be confronted with
  BOTH registered constraints: (a) Malus — the direction must live in the
  transverse plane as a two-component vector whose projection gives cos²θ;
  (b) consistency with the static radial direction under the same definition.
  A miss on either is registered, not excused.
- **N4 (honest underdetermination)**: if the registered commitments do not fix
  the direction, the outcome is the UNDERDETERMINATION, registered with the
  minimal missing commitment named — not a quiet adoption.
- **N5 (carrier honesty)**: the commission does NOT resurrect any killed
  carrier. If the derivation is conditional on a carrier class, the condition
  is stated on the face and the claim's strength is capped at the condition.

## Outcome space (pre-enumerated)
1. Direction derived unconditionally from the operational definition.
2. Direction derived CONDITIONAL on a carrier class (e.g., any carrier that
   displaces center lines transversely) — the condition registered.
3. Underdetermined — the minimal missing commitment named and registered.
4. Contradiction between the operational definition and a registered
   constraint — registered as a failure at full volume.
