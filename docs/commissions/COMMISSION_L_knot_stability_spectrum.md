## The convergence this commission is built on
Three facts from the campaign point at one object:
- F4 proved the ratio route is blocked by the PROTON, not the electron
  and not the calibration: no registered claim assigns the proton a
  topology, and 1836 needs ~1000 crossings the certified solver cannot
  reach. The electron was never the blocker.
- The corpus computes COUNTS well and absolute magnitudes badly:
  nucleon count for nuclear mass (NUC-001), mode count for the first
  three nuclear magic numbers (NUC-007: 2, 8, 20 from isotropic
  mode-counting; 28 MISSED, spin-orbit unbuilt), capacity count for
  alpha-clustering and the N=Z symmetry sign (NUC-008/009), crossing
  count for the ratio target (F4).
- The knot solver (FND-MATTER-006) and a certified spectrum (ring,
  3_1, 4_1, 5_1, granny, square, ...) EXIST but have never been asked
  whether their STABILITY ORDERING reproduces any observed sequence.

The hypothesis this commission tests: ONE counting/stability rule on the
knot spectrum may generate the proton's structure, the m_p/m_e ratio,
and an observed periodicity TOGETHER -- the same way NUC-007 got 2, 8, 20
from one mode-counting rule. If so, "what is the proton," "why 1836," and
"why the stability sequence" are one question with one answer in knot
topology. If not, the failure prunes the hypothesis and maps which of the
three the knot spectrum can and cannot carry.

## Scope discipline, stated first (this is the commission's main risk)
This is a SEQUENCE-and-STRUCTURE question, run dimensionlessly on the
registered solver, NOT a bid to derive absolute masses (F1 proved that
road circular) and NOT a licence to fit. The entire value is in whether
an ordering falls out UNFORCED. The look-elsewhere risk is acute: knot
invariants are many (crossing number, ropelength, genus, determinant,
hyperbolic volume, bridge number, ...) and observed sequences are several
(nuclear magic 2/8/20/28/50/82/126; alpha-multiples 4/8/12/16; the single
ratio 1836). A rule found by scanning invariants against sequences until
one pairs is worthless. The bars enforce commit-before-compare at every
level.

## PHASE 1: THE STABILITY RULE, DEFINED BEFORE ANY SEQUENCE IS LOADED

### The question
Is there a rule on the knot spectrum -- expressible in registered solver
quantities (ropelength L, the registered zero-point column dE_zp, and
topological receipts already certified per FND-MATTER-015) -- that
assigns each knot a STABILITY, such that the stable structures fall in a
non-arbitrary ordered sequence?

### Bars
- L1 (COMMIT THE RULE FIRST): the stability rule is written down and
  frozen BEFORE any observed sequence (magic numbers, alpha-multiples,
  1836) is loaded into the session. The rule must be motivated by
  registered mechanics (e.g. "a structure is stable when its ropelength
  per unit topological charge is a local minimum," or "when its contact
  capacity is saturated," NUC-007's q=3 logic lifted to knots) -- NOT by
  what would reproduce a target. The motivation is recorded; a rule whose
  only justification is its output is void.
- L2 (ONE RULE, NOT A FAMILY): a single stability functional is
  committed. Scanning a family of functionals for the one that fits is
  the look-elsewhere failure and is forbidden by name. If the first
  committed rule fails, that failure is REGISTERED before any second rule
  is proposed, each under its own future charter (the F4/K3 precedent).
- L3 (INVARIANT DECLARED, NOT SHOPPED): the one topological invariant
  the rule uses is declared with the rule. Crossing number is the
  natural first candidate (it is what F4's ~1000-crossing demand is
  stated in, and what NUC-007's mode-counting is morally close to);
  if a different invariant is chosen, the choice is justified from
  mechanics before the sequence is seen.

## PHASE 2: THE THREE CONFRONTATIONS (sealed; sequences loaded only after
## Phase 1 commits)

Run in this order, each a separate verdict, no aggregation:

- L-A (THE SEQUENCE): does the committed rule's stable-structure ordering
  reproduce ANY observed periodicity without adjustment? Two pre-named
  targets, both dimensionless: the nuclear magic numbers (2, 8, 20, and
  whether the rule ALSO misses at 28 like NUC-007, which would be a
  CONSILIENCE -- the same spin-orbit gap surfacing from knot topology as
  from mode-counting, strong evidence the rule is real), and the
  alpha-multiple periodicity (4, 8, 12, 16; NUC-009 recovered these from
  cluster geometry, so the knot rule reproducing them is a cross-check
  against an already-Modeled result).
- L-B (THE PROTON): does the rule single out a specific structure as the
  nucleon? The FND-MATTER-007 fork named the enormous-tangle tine;
  L-B asks whether the stability rule POINTS at a crossing-number class
  for the nucleon, even if the exact knot is beyond the certified solver.
  A rule that says "the first stable structure above the mesonic floor
  sits near N crossings" is a registrable structural prediction even
  without the thousand-crossing solver, and it either matches F4's
  ~1000-crossing demand (consilience) or contradicts it (a real internal
  tension to register).
- L-C (THE RATIO, gated on L-B): ONLY if L-B points at a nucleon class,
  form the dimensionless ratio of that class's ropelength (extrapolated
  along the rule's own stability curve, with the extrapolation's grade
  stated) against the electron floor (the ring, or whatever F-programme
  identity survives). Compare to 1836 under F4's pre-committed bar
  (3x whisper, 25 percent promotion). This is F4 re-attempted from the
  PROTON side with a stability rule supplying the structure F4 lacked --
  the honest closing of F4's gate IF the rule delivers, and a clean
  negative if it does not.

### Phase 2 bars
- L4 (SEQUENCES DIMENSIONLESS): every confrontation is a pure-number
  comparison; no absolute scale enters (B3 inherited from F4, exhibited
  in the benchmark).
- L5 (CONSILIENCE IS THE PRIZE, NOT A SINGLE HIT): the strongest
  registrable outcome is the SAME rule landing L-A and L-B and L-C
  together -- one counting rule generating sequence, proton, and ratio.
  A single hit (say L-A alone) is registered at the look-elsewhere grade
  MATTER055 established: one sequence matched by one rule among the
  invariant choices is weak; three matched by one committed rule is the
  result. The bar pre-weights the outcomes so a lone L-A hit cannot be
  oversold.
- L6 (THE 28 MISS IS A FEATURE): if the rule reproduces 2, 8, 20 and
  MISSES 28 exactly as NUC-007 does, that is registered as CONSILIENCE
  (two independent routes to the same spin-orbit boundary), strengthening
  the rule, not weakening it. Conversely a rule that GETS 28 without a
  spin mechanism is treated with suspicion (it may be fitting), and its
  extra success is audited before it is believed.

## Registrable outcomes (all acceptable)
1. FULL CONSILIENCE: one committed rule lands L-A, L-B, L-C. The proton
   is a knot-stability structure, 1836 is its ratio, and the stability
   sequence is a knot-spectrum property. This would be the matter
   sector's largest positive result and would reframe F1-F4 from "the
   identity is blocked" to "the identity is a stability class." Registered
   at the grade the weakest confrontation supports (likely the L-C
   extrapolation).
2. SEQUENCE ONLY (L-A lands, L-B/L-C do not): the stability rule captures
   nuclear periodicity but does not single out the proton or the ratio.
   Registered at look-elsewhere grade with the caveat at full volume;
   the rule is a nuclear-structure finding, not a mass result.
3. PROTON-CLASS ONLY (L-B lands, L-A/L-C do not): the rule points at a
   nucleon crossing-class matching F4's ~1000 demand but does not
   reproduce the sequence. A consilience with F4 worth registering; the
   ratio and periodicity stay open.
4. CLEAN NEGATIVE: the committed rule lands none. The hypothesis that one
   counting rule unifies proton/ratio/sequence is pruned for THIS rule
   and THIS invariant; L2/L3 govern any successor. The knot spectrum's
   inability to carry the sequence is itself a map entry (the F4 gate is
   confirmed as needing genuinely new solver physics, not just a
   stability reading of existing structures).
5. FIT DETECTED (the discipline outcome): if the session finds itself
   needing to shop invariants or rules to land anything, it STOPS and
   registers "no unforced rule found," which is the honest content and
   prevents the look-elsewhere landscape from producing a false positive.

## Seal and sequencing
Phase 1 (rule + invariant commit) is one session, self-sealed by L1-L3
(the sequences are not in the room). Phase 2 is a second session
receiving the frozen rule and the sequences. L-C is gated on L-B within
Phase 2. No session runs both phases. The B4 web-verification protocol
does not apply (no external observational bound beyond the textbook
sequences, which are not contested and may be stated from standard
nuclear data with a citation).

## Stopping rule
One rule, one invariant, three confrontations, one registration. No
invariant-scanning, no rule-family search (L2/L3/L5). A negative prunes
and is kept. Successor rules are future charters, named not opened.

## What this commission is and is not
IS: the proton-side mirror of the electron identity programme, testing
whether knot-stability topology unifies the proton, the mass ratio, and
the stability sequence -- Mark's twin question made computable on
registered instruments.
IS NOT: a derivation of absolute masses (F1-blocked), a fit (L1/L2/L5-
barred), or a bid to build the thousand-crossing solver (that remains the
named prerequisite for a full L-C; this commission tests whether the
stability CURVE can be extrapolated to it, which is a weaker and
achievable thing).
