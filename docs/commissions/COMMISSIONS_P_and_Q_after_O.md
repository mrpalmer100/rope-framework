===============================================================================

## The question
O named it: does the transverse polarization DOUBLET -- the two-state
transverse structure the photon needed (the carrier campaign, I/J) --
supply the spin-degeneracy factor of 2 CLASSICALLY, removing bottleneck
(2) from the QUANTUM_LEDGER entirely?

## Why it matters and why it is cheap
The ledger's four quantum inputs are electron mass, DEGENERACY (2), the
13.6 eV coupling, and the induced-G normalization. O falsified unification
across all four-plus-occupancy. But if the degeneracy 2 is not a quantum
input at all -- if it is the classical transverse doublet -- then the
ledger drops to three inputs and O's negative is re-confronted in a
REDUCED sector. This is the one bottleneck with a plausible classical
origin already sitting in the corpus (the two transverse polarizations,
which the carrier campaign established the medium must carry), so it is
the cheapest possible shot at shrinking the quantum sector.

## The structural echo (the reason to believe it might land)
The carrier campaign (Commission I) proved the photon needs TWO genuine
transverse polarization states with independent phase (the C^2 / Poincare
structure). Spin degeneracy is also a factor of 2. If these are the SAME
2 -- if the mode's two transverse polarizations ARE its two spin states --
then the degeneracy in NUC-007's capacity 2(2l+1) and M's closure rule is
classical geometry, not a quantum input. The (2l+1) is already classical
(rotational, the spherical harmonics); the question is only the leading 2.

## Bars
- P1 (THE 2 MUST BE THE DOUBLET, not just numerically 2): the claim
  requires showing the degeneracy-2 in the occupation rule is the SAME
  two-state structure as the transverse polarization doublet -- same
  states, same counting -- not merely that both happen to equal 2.
  A numerical coincidence of two 2's is not a derivation (the
  look-elsewhere discipline: many things equal 2).
- P2 (CLASSICAL PROVENANCE): the doublet's two states must be exhibited
  as classical (the transverse polarization plane, no hbar), consistent
  with the carrier channel-map. If supplying the degeneracy requires the
  quantum spin-statistics connection, the 2 is NOT classical and P fails.
- P3 (LEDGER UPDATE): if P lands, the QUANTUM_LEDGER is amended from four
  inputs to three, and O's unification question is RE-OPENED in the
  reduced sector as a named next-order (does one constant serve three?).
  If P fails, the 2 stays a quantum input and the ledger is unchanged.

## Outcomes
1. The degeneracy 2 IS the transverse doublet, classical: ledger drops to
   three inputs, O reduced-sector reopened. A real narrowing.
2. The 2 is numerically the doublet but requires spin-statistics (quantum)
   to connect occupation to polarization: registered as "structural
   suggestion, quantum connection required" -- the 2 stays quantum.
3. No match: the occupation-2 and the polarization-2 are unrelated; the
   degeneracy stays an independent quantum input; clean negative.

Q's target: fix the confinement scale from mechanics (spending neither m_e

vacuum-tension/M-point pattern applied to the quantum sector: find the one
