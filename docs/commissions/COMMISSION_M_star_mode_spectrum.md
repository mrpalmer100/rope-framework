## PHASE 1: THE STAR-MODE SPECTRUM (compute the modes; hbar-blind)

### The question
Solve the standing-wave (normal-mode) spectrum of the strand-star
convergence in the registered geometry (R ~ a sqrt(N), N from
FND-MATTER-004's coverage threshold, the medium's registered wave
operator from EM-RECON / GRV-029). Report the mode spectrum as a set of
dimensionless eigenvalue RATIOS (each mode's frequency over the ground
mode's), which is the hbar-independent content -- eigenvalue ratios of a
wave operator carry no absolute scale and no hbar.

### Bars
- M1 (RATIOS ONLY, hbar-BLIND): the deliverable is the spectrum of
  dimensionless eigenvalue ratios omega_k/omega_0. No absolute frequency,
  no hbar, no mass in kg/eV appears (B3-style exhibit in the benchmark).
  The wave operator's absolute stiffness cancels in every ratio by
  construction; the session verifies this cancellation explicitly.
- M2 (GEOMETRY FROM REGISTERED CLAIMS): the confining geometry (the
  star's boundary, the coverage threshold N, the effective potential or
  index profile the converging ropes present) is taken from
  FND-MATTER-002/004 and the registered medium properties, NOT chosen to
  produce a target. Any modeling choice (boundary condition at the core,
  at the coverage radius) is declared and justified from mechanics before
  the spectrum is computed, and alternatives are reported, not selected.
- M3 (VALIDATION): the mode solver is validated on a geometry with a
  known analytic spectrum (a uniform spherical cavity, whose eigenvalue
  ratios are standard) before the star geometry is trusted -- the
  FND-MATTER-006 unknot-control discipline. Control result and error
  reported.
- M4 (CLOSURE CONDITION DECLARED): the criterion that makes a mode STABLE
  / occupied (the analogue of a full shell) is committed before the
  spectrum is read for structure -- e.g. a coverage-threshold closure (a
  mode is stable when its amplitude profile saturates the tangibility
  threshold), or a nodal/topological closure. One criterion, motivated
  from mechanics, not scanned (the L2 discipline).

Sequences and targets loaded only after Phase 1's spectrum commits.

### Phase 2 bars
- M5 (THE hbar VERDICT IS MANDATORY, whichever way it falls): the session

  expressible in mode arithmetic ALONE, or whether a step required
  importing hbar / an absolute scale. If mode-arithmetic-only: this is
