# COMMISSION D: THE ANISOTROPIC FAMILY COUNT

## The question
The lambda construction (MATTER056/058) treats the displaced zero-point
content isotropically: one number, pi r^2/a^2, for all modes. But the
inclusion is a strand LYING ALONG one of the three orthogonal weave
families, and the geometry it presents is family-dependent:

- To the two PERPENDICULAR families, it is a small cylindrical obstacle of
  cross-section pi r^2 per crossing cell.
- To its OWN family, it is not an inclusion at all; it is a member. Modes
  of the parallel family propagating along the same axis see a fundamentally
  different geometry (a neighboring strand, not a transverse obstacle).

The current lambda averages over this structure without ever computing it.
Task: compute the displaced zero-point fraction PER FAMILY from the
geometry, and assemble the correct weighted total.

## What this is and is not
This is NOT the voided directional share. FND-MATTER-060 killed a
PARTITION factor inside a single ratio (both sides of a fraction must use
one unit of account; the 3 cancels). This commission computes DIFFERENT
NUMERATORS for different mode populations, each over its own correctly
matched denominator. Every per-family fraction must independently satisfy
the 060 bookkeeping rule: numerator and denominator in the same units of
account, any content density cancelling identically. The 060 verdict is a
constraint on this construction, not an obstacle to it.

## Pre-committed bars
- B1 (the 060 gate, checked first): each per-family fraction is exhibited
  in the form (displaced content)/(available content) with a symbolic
  cancellation check of an arbitrary content density nu, per the
  matter060 benchmark's method. Any fraction failing this check voids the
  construction immediately.
- B2: the parallel-family geometry must be posed and solved as its own
  mode problem, not assigned a fraction by analogy with the perpendicular
  case. If the parallel contribution is zero or ill-posed, that is a
  reportable result, not a defect.
- B3: the family weights (how much of the ambient band belongs to each
  family at the inclusion's location) must come from registered weave
  structure (FND-017's per-direction family count is the natural source,
  used HERE in its legitimate role: counting mode populations, not
  repartitioning a ratio).
- B4: target-blind, two-session seal, derive-then-compare, comparison bar
  factor 2.00, one comparison. Identical protocol to Commission C.
- B5: independence from Commission C enforced: this commission uses the
  displaced-fraction ansatz WITHIN each family (the thing C is testing) so
  that the two commissions vary one assumption each. If both land, the
  combined construction is a THIRD computation with its own registration;
  the factors may not be multiplied informally.

## Stopping rule
One geometry analysis, one per-family computation, one assembly, one
comparison. No iteration on the family weights after the comparison.

## Registrable outcomes (all acceptable)
1. The anisotropic total lands inside the bar: registered as a derived
   lever candidate, graded by the weakest link (likely the within-family
   ansatz, pending C).
2. It moves the value away: the isotropic approximation was flattering the
   mechanism; the displacement class weakens; keeper.
3. The parallel-family problem proves ill-posed in the dilute picture:
   registered as a structural finding about what "displacement" can mean
   on a weave, feeding C's boundary-condition question.
