## The question, and why this configuration is now the favorite
G killed the displacement carrier propagating INDEPENDENTLY (massive,
superluminal). H killed the bare torsion carrier on state count, made
physical by the guitar-string objections now on Commission I's face
(one azimuthal direction; the corkscrew). Notice the SHAPE of the two
negatives: the displacement sector has the honest two transverse
directions but the wrong dynamics; the torsion sector has exactly the
right dynamics (massless by theorem, exact light cone, crossing-blind)
but apparently one direction. Each carrier has exactly what the other
lacks. That complementarity points at the one configuration nobody has
computed: the DRESSED carrier -- the protected screw as the propagating
core setting the clock, with the massive displacement sector dragged
along as slaved, off-shell dressing through the derived twist-stretch
lock (gamma = 1/sin^2(theta), EM-RECON-012).

The physical statement being tested: a massive sector cannot propagate
on its own -- that is what G established -- but it CAN be forced at the
frequency of a gapless carrier passing through, the way a driven
oscillator responds below resonance. Twist locks to stretch; stretch
lives in the displacement sector; the displacement sector owns the two
transverse dimensions. If the forced dressing is where the observable
field strength lives, the composite could inherit masslessness and
exact c from the screw AND two-dimensional polarization from the
cloud. Whether it does is a computation, and the charter states the
favorite status so the deriving session's bars can be read as guarding
against exactly that enthusiasm (dressing is not a generator; the
GRV-018 counterexample spec is not invoked; nothing below is loosened
because the candidate is liked).

## The computation, on H's registered instrument
H already built the coupled Phi-u stiffness matrix and proved its
k-independence entry by entry (the lock cannot manufacture dispersion).
This commission asks the SAME matrix the next question:

  (i)   solve the coupled system ON the Phi light cone: for a screw
        wave of wavevector q, amplitude Phi_0, what u-components are
        forced, at what amplitude ratio u/Phi_0, with what transverse
        geometry (direction, phase relative to the twist)?
  (ii)  the dressing's energetics: what fraction of the propagating
        energy rides in the u-cloud vs the torsional core, as a
        function of q -- and does the composite's group velocity
        remain exactly c (it must, if H's k-independence theorem is as
        stated; this is a consistency check with teeth, since a
        dressed mode with energy in a massive sector generically
        disperses UNLESS the lock's structure forbids it, and
        "computed, not argued" applies).
  (iii) the crossing response of the DRESSED object: H proved the bare
        screw is azimuth-blind at crossings (dE_c = 0 identically).
        The u-cloud is NOT azimuth-blind -- it is exactly the sector
        the crossings couple to at O(g). Compute the dressed mode's
        crossing coupling: does the cloud reintroduce a mass, a
        g^2-tooth, or an attenuation the bare screw was immune to? The
        protection theorem covers the generator, not its dressing, and
        this is where the rescue most plausibly dies. Advance
        statement per the H4(c) discipline: if the dressing's crossing
        coupling scales as (u/Phi_0)^2 x O(g) and u/Phi_0 is itself
        O(lock strength), the induced effects may sit far below the
        displacement carrier's kills -- whether "far below" clears
        PDG's m_gamma < 1e-18 eV and the LHAASO bounds is the
        confrontation.
  (iv)  the transverse structure delivered: does the forced u-geometry
        provide the second real dimension that kill class 4 found
        missing? Three sub-questions, each computed: (iv-a) the
        DIMENSION count -- is the forced u a genuine two-component
        transverse field supporting independent superposition, or is
        its direction slaved to the twist phase (one dimension wearing
        the cloud's clothes)? (iv-b) THE PITCH AVERAGE (Mark's
        corkscrew objection, transferred from Commission I): the
        standing pitch tau0 rotates the rope's internal frame at
        sub-nuclear scale, so any u-geometry tied to the local frame
        corkscrews ~1e6+ times per optical wavelength; the dressed
        mode survives only if the OBSERVABLE transverse direction is
        set by something pitch-invariant (e.g. the forcing wave's own
        envelope or an external-frame component of u), and whether it
        is must be read off the solution, not asserted. A dressing
        whose direction pitch-averages to isotropy fails Malus and
        the outcome-4 verdict fires. (iv-c) the dictionary hand-off:
        this verdict line is subordinated to Commission I -- if I has
