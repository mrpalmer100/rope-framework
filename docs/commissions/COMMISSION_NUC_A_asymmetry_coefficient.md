# COMMISSION NUC-A: THE ASYMMETRY COEFFICIENT FROM MODE STATISTICS
# (chartered 2026-08-06. Tier one of the 13%-gap pursuit, following the
# clean tier-zero audit. The audit proved the heavy-table binding gap is
# ONE effect with the exact predicted shape: Fermi asymmetry (N-Z)^2/A
# plus a parity-flipping pairing term, 98.8% of variance across 279
# nuclides. This commission attempts to DERIVE the asymmetry coefficient
# a_A from the framework's own mode-counting -- the same machinery that
# already produced the magic numbers 2, 8, 20. GENEROUS bars on the
# science; a HARD cap on the scope.)

## The target (sharp, from the audit)
Produce, from strand mode statistics, the nuclear symmetry-energy
coefficient a_A. The audit's committed two-term fit gave a_A = 19.26 MeV
(volume value, standard without a surface-asymmetry cross term; empirical
effective 23.2). The asymmetry term is the BULK of the 13% heavy-table
gap, so deriving a_A converts most of that gap from omission to derived
physics.

## Why this is bounded, not a doorway into the quantum campaign
The framework ALREADY counts modes in a confining well and got the magic
numbers 2, 8, 20 from it (NUC-007). The symmetry energy is the SAME
physics: the energy cost of filling proton and neutron levels UNEQUALLY
is a state-counting argument in that same well. This commission EXTENDS
registered machinery to a new observable; it does NOT derive Fermi
statistics from scratch. That is what keeps it a single bounded
commission rather than a campaign.

## THE BARS -- GENEROUS ON THE SCIENCE (the LEAD-2 lesson applied)
The failure mode this charter explicitly guards against: bars set so
tight that a right-shape result with an off magnitude gets killed before
its second prediction runs. That mistake is forbidden here.
- SUCCESS IS WIDE: a_A landing anywhere in the physically sensible range
  17-27 MeV counts as a hit (wider than the audit's 19-23, because the
  volume/surface split moves the coefficient around and the audit itself
  flagged this). The point is to derive the coefficient at the right
  scale, not to hit 23.2 on the nose.
- RIGHT SHAPE, OFF MAGNITUDE = A LEAD, NOT A FAILURE: if the mode-counting
  produces the correct (N-Z)^2/A dependence but a coefficient outside
  17-27, that is a LIVE LEAD, kept, pursued to a SECOND PREDICTION before
  any verdict. Nothing dies on one number being off.
- THE SECOND PREDICTION (the arbiter): a candidate mode-counting derivation
  that gives a_A must then predict something INDEPENDENT with no new
  freedom -- the pairing term's SIGN (even-even most bound), or the
  magic-number level spacing it already knows, or the A-dependence of the
  symmetry energy. A candidate that predicts a second thing GRADUATES even
  if a_A was off; one that predicts nothing further stays a lead or dies.
- LOOK HARD, KEEP CANDIDATES: if the cleanest mode-counting (uniform well)
  misses, the session MAY explore variants -- different well profiles,
  surface corrections, the derived nucleon spacing -- keeping every
  candidate with the right shape and pursuing each to its second
  prediction. This is the generous exploration Mark authorized; it is not
  "compute once and declare failure."
- BLIND WHERE IT MATTERS: the mode-counting is set up from the well
  geometry and the derived nucleon spacing BEFORE a_A's empirical value is
  used to grade it. The coefficient is an output compared to the range,
  not a target fitted to.

## THE SCOPE CAP -- HARD (this is where cost is controlled)
Cost is bounded by SCOPE, not by tight science bars:
- This commission is the ASYMMETRY COEFFICIENT, singular. Explore it
  thoroughly.
- The PAIRING term (a_P) and the SHELL-STRUCTURE residual (~6 MeV rms, the
  magic-number survivors) are EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE. They are the audit's
  named tier-two and tier-three, and they require a SEPARATE, DELIBERATE
  go-decision from Mark -- they are NOT auto-spawned by this session, no
  matter how tempting. A session that finishes the asymmetry work and
  starts on pairing has violated the scope cap.
- No sub-commissions. If the asymmetry work suggests follow-ons, they are
  NAMED for Mark's decision, not opened.
This is the discipline that caps token cost: generous exploration WITHIN
the asymmetry term, a hard wall around it.

## PHASE STRUCTURE (one session, or two if exploration warrants)
- PRIMARY: set up proton/neutron level-filling in the framework's
  confining well (NUC-007's machinery), compute the energy cost of unequal
  filling as a function of (N-Z) and A, extract the coefficient of
  (N-Z)^2/A. Compare to 17-27 MeV.
- IF IT LANDS: register a_A DERIVED, run one second prediction to confirm
  (pairing sign or magic spacing), done.
- IF RIGHT-SHAPE OFF-MAGNITUDE: keep as lead, explore the named variants,
  pursue each to a second prediction, register the best-supported outcome.
- IF NO (N-Z)^2/A SHAPE EMERGES AT ALL: register the honest negative (the
  mode-counting does not produce the symmetry energy), which locates the
  boundary precisely and is a real result. Stop.

## Registrable outcomes (all acceptable)
1. DERIVED: mode-counting gives a_A in 17-27 MeV with the right shape, and
   a second prediction confirms. The bulk of the 13% gap is now derived
   physics; the symmetry energy joins the magic numbers as a mode-counting
   result. Register the new binding accuracy across the table.
2. DERIVED-WITH-CORRECTION: a_A lands after a named, mechanically-justified
   variant (e.g. surface correction), with a second prediction holding.
   Still a real derivation, the correction stated.
3. LEAD (right shape, magnitude off, second prediction partial): registered
   as a live lead with its exact miss and its next test named for a future
   go-decision. Not a failure.
4. BOUNDARY (no symmetry-energy shape from mode-counting): the honest
   negative -- the framework's mode-counting does not extend to the
   symmetry energy, locating where the quantum sector genuinely enters.
   Registered; the 13% asymmetry stays a declared omission, now with the
   boundary sharpened.
