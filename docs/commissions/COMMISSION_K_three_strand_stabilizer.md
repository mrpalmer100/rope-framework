# COMMISSION K: THE THREE-STRAND STABILIZER
# (chartered 2026-08-05; GATED on Commissions I and J closing negative
# for the two-strand ontology. The ontology-extension question, run as
# the corpus's own pre-registered counterexample spec rather than as a
# rescue invented for the occasion.)

## Standing, and why this is not a rescue postulate
If I confirms kill class 4 and J fails to dress past it, the registered
two-strand ontology provably cannot carry light, and the photon-carrier
problem becomes a demand for structure the ontology does not have. The
corpus has ALREADY written the spec for that structure, two eras ago,
under external attack, for reasons having nothing to do with the
photon: GRV-018's sharpened counterexample specification -- "a
substrate whose internal continuous symmetry group has AT LEAST TWO
generators, with the second neutrally sourceable" -- with the explicit
open question "whether richer internal structure (n-strand braids,
internally structured strands) can provide a second." The photon needs
exactly two states. The consilience of a defensively written spec and
an independently arising demand is the motivation; per the standing
discipline it is motivation, NOT evidence, and this charter's outcomes
include the consilience dissolving on computation.

The postulate-audit standard (the P-VOL precedent: necessity AND
independent naturalness AND consistency) is addressed on the face:
necessity is supplied by I+J's negatives; independent naturalness by
the spec predating the demand; consistency is what this commission
computes.

## The computation
Extend GRV-020's formal machinery -- the stabilizer computation that
proved the two-strand rope's internal continuous symmetry group is
G/H = S^1 with one Goldstone, and the angular no-monopole lemma -- to
the three-strand braid (and, if tractable at the same grade, n-strand
generally):

  (i)   the stabilizer: compute the internal continuous symmetry group
        of the tight three-strand braid's cross-sectional
        configuration. The two-strand answer was one generator (the
        screw; the discrete strand-exchange changed a quotient, not
        the count). Three strands at the vertices of a rotating
        triangle admit candidate continuous families (collective
        rotation = the screw analogue; and whatever internal
        deformations survive the tightness constraint) -- the count is
        the result, and 1, 2, and >2 are all live.
  (ii)  gaplessness and protection: for each generator found, the
        Goldstone construction explicit (which deformations cost zero
        at q -> 0) and the crossing-blindness check (H's azimuth
        identity rerun for each mode: does a sub-threshold contact,
        constraining centerline separations, see the mode?). A second
        generator that crossings CAN see is gapped by the FND-REL-005
        mechanism and does not count.
  (iii) neutral sourceability: GRV-016/018's triple closure barred
        neutral masses from sourcing the two-strand screw at monopole
        order. The second generator, if found, must be checkable
        against the same bar -- the spec requires neutrally
        sourceable, and this charter carries the check rather than
        assuming it.
  (iv)  THE HARD CONSTRAINT, charge conservation: GG-006's charge =
        winding/linking is Derived and load-bearing across the corpus
        (Gauss law, current, the entire EM topology). The three-strand
        braid's topological invariants differ from the two-strand
        rope's (braid group B_3 vs B_2; the winding that WAS charge
        must either persist as one of B_3's invariants or the
        extension is DEAD ON ARRIVAL regardless of its generator
        count. This check runs FIRST, before the stabilizer, because
        it is the cheapest kill and it is wrong-wherever-it-lands
        (target-free in the METHODOLOGY sense): what B_3's invariants
        are is a mathematical fact with no convenient answer.
  (v)   collateral survey, if (i)-(iv) pass: the two-strand rope is
        load-bearing EVERYWHERE (the lock's gamma = 1/sin^2 theta is
        two-strand geometry; EM-RECON-012's derivations; the GRV
        sector's mode band; the tangibility threshold's coverage
        arithmetic). A survey pass names every registered claim whose
        derivation is n = 2 specific, in the four-column style --
        NOT to redo them, but to price the extension honestly: an
        ontology change that rescues the photon and silently orphans
        the gravity sector is not a rescue. The survey is a mandatory
        deliverable of a positive outcome.

## Bars
- K1 (ORDERING): (iv) charge first, then (i)-(iii), then the survey.
  The confrontation numbers (what the photon needs) enter only after
  the generator count and its protection status are committed --
  standard clean-packet split; this preamble is the contamination
  surface.
- K2 (GRADE): the stabilizer computation is registrable at Derived
  grade only if it reaches GRV-020's own formality (explicit group
  action, explicit quotient, machine-checked where the two-strand case
  was). A plausibility count is registrable at Conjecture and gates
  nothing.
- K3 (NO SHOPPING): n = 3 is the committed candidate, pre-registered
  here because it is the minimal extension. If it fails, the failure
  is registered before any n = 4+ or structured-strand candidate is
  examined, each under its own future charter -- a scan across n until
  one passes is the look-elsewhere failure mode and is forbidden by
  name.
- K4 (THE SPEC IS THE JUDGE): the pass condition is GRV-018's spec
  verbatim -- at least two generators, second neutrally sourceable,
  now plus crossing-blind (the H extension) and charge-preserving (the
  GG-006 constraint). Partial passes (two generators, one crossing-
  visible) are registered as the finding they are, not negotiated.
- K5 (BOTH SECTORS AT THE TABLE): a positive outcome's registration
  must state its consequence for the gravity no-go IN THE SAME CLAIM:
  GRV-018 defended H1 by showing two strands provide one generator; a
  substrate with two generators is exactly the door H1 guards, so a
  photon-rescuing extension REOPENS the gravity sector's defense
  perimeter, and the second generator's neutral-sourceability verdict
  from (iii) decides how wide. The corpus does not get the photon back
  by quietly un-defending gravity; the coupled ledger is the price of
  admission and this bar makes it undodgeable.

## Registrable outcomes (all acceptable)
1. B_3 preserves charge, the stabilizer yields a second protected,
   crossing-blind, neutrally-unsourceable-at-monopole generator: the
   minimal extension exists; registered at K2's earned grade; the
   collateral survey and the K5 gravity statement owed in full; a
   successor commission (the three-strand photon: dispersion,
   polarization dictionary, full G/H-protocol confrontation) is named
   but NOT opened here.
2. Charge dies in B_3 ((iv) fails): the minimal extension is dead on
   arrival; registered as the cheapest kill working as designed; K3
   governs any successor candidate.
3. The count stays at one (collective rotation is again the only
   continuous family): GRV-018's open question closes NEGATIVE for
   n = 3; the demand for structure sharpens (whatever supplies the
   second generator, it is not braid multiplicity at n = 3); K3
   governs.
4. Two generators, wrong properties (crossing-visible, or chargeable):
   partial pass registered per K4; the spec's remaining freedom
   measured, which is itself the GRV-018 door's dimensions updated.
5. The computation exceeds the session's formality reach: registered
   at Conjecture per K2, gating nothing, with the missing formal step
   named -- the honest "the corpus cannot yet decide this" outcome.

## Stopping rule
One charge check, one stabilizer computation, one protection/
sourceability pass per generator found, one survey on positive
outcome. No n-scanning (K3). Successor commissions named, never opened
from within this one.
