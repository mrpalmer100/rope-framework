# Closure record: the mesoscopic-hbar identification

Filed 2026-08-01 (ELEC-061). Read this before any individual HBAR claim.

## What is closed

The identification of hbar's action length with a MESOSCOPIC PATCH of the
strand medium -- HBAR-005's 4.31 fm, roughly seventy-five spacings across, and
HBAR-006's sub-quantum cell built on it. That identification produced the
corpus's only claim modified gravity could not make: that Born statistics
should fail near the nuclear scale. It is withdrawn.

## The six closures

1. **Nuclear data** (NUCQ-001). The prediction places the entire nuclear chart
   inside one sub-quantum grain, where the shell model, ab initio no-core
   calculations and lattice QCD achieve percent-level agreement using ordinary
   quantum mechanics. No reading passes.
2. **The Lorentz bound, unaided** (ELEC-057). Fitting even ONE patch inside
   He-4 requires a strand scale 6.75x LARGER than the bound permits. Two of
   the framework's own registered constraints contradict each other, with no
   reference to nuclear data at all.
3. **Every strand scale** (ELEC-057). The one free length was swept over its
   full allowed range. The electron and hbar sectors' requirements pass each
   other 5.2 decades apart without touching.
4. **Bundling** (ELEC-058). Recovering the needed tension from many small
   strands fails: the causal recruitment budget is scale-invariant while the
   required coherence radius is fixed by measurement, so the shortfall grows
   as 1/a -- 3.46 at the ceiling, 7.3e4 where the electron needs it.
5. **Coherent-unit width** (ELEC-059). A wider unit does not help: the
   diameter cancels exactly, since widening lengthens the causal reach and
   enlarges the required radius in the same proportion. Only constituent
   count matters, and closing needs 12 strands per unit against the
   electron's two.
6. **The two-medium option** (ELEC-060). Splitting vacuum from matter rescues
   the electron only; the hbar sector's failure is against the Lorentz bound,
   which constrains the vacuum whatever else it shares its world with. The
   sub-branch that would free the vacuum tension does so by handing away the
   corpus's only external anchor, converting a refuted sector into an
   unfalsifiable one. Declined by name.

Closures 2 through 6 required no new experimental input.

## What SURVIVES, and is not touched

- **HBAR-001's standing-wave FORM**, S = pi T A^2/(2c). Verified directly:
  identical at L = 1 fm, 1 pm, 1 nm and 1 m. The length cancels, so the form
  is untouched by anything above. What was never derived, and remains open,
  is what SELECTS the amplitude (ELEC-054).
- **Six kept negatives** (HBAR-002, -003, -004, -007, -008, -009). A mechanism
  that fails to select a scale fails whether or not the scale was viable.
  These keep their status and their lessons.
- **HBAR-010**, independent of the patch entirely: a comoving medium is
  excluded by five orders, the medium must be rigid to one part in 2e5, and
  the framework inherits a testable preferred frame.
- **NUCQ-001**, which is UPHELD by this closure rather than overturned, and
  **NUCQ-002's** structural/coherence firewall.

## What the corpus LOSES

The prediction of nuclear-scale Born-statistics violation, and with it the
corpus's only distinctive testable claim -- the deficit NUCQ-001 itself first
recorded. **CORRECTION (ELEC-062, same day):** the sentence above says the corpus's ONLY
distinctive testable claim. That was the standing assessment inherited from
NUCQ-001 and it is wrong. A systematic census found THREE surviving
predictions -- GRV-039, HBAR-010 and QGATE-007/010 -- none in the quantum or
matter sectors. What is lost here is the quantum sector's only distinctive
prediction, not the corpus's.

The pilot-wave branch (QGATE-011..017) was separately shown by
ELEC-056 never to have depended on this length, so it is unaffected in its
mathematics and remains, as registered, without empirical content of its own.

## Reopening

Would require a derivation of hbar's amplitude scale that does not place a
mesoscopic grain inside nuclei, and would have to clear the Lorentz-bound
contradiction (closure 2) first, since that one needs no experiment to say no.
