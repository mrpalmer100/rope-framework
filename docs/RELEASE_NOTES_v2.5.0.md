# v2.5.0 — The chain from the electron to the sky, and the first prediction that held

Corpus state: **313 registered claims (102 Derived, 180 Modeled, 4 EFT-constrained, 3
Conjecture, 5 Open, 19 Failed-and-kept); 299 benchmark-backed claims, all passing; 60 papers.**

Thirty-three claims and two new families. A minor version, because something happened that
had not happened before: the corpus committed to a number in advance, named the data that
would settle it, and the data agreed. The path there ran through the electron campaign's
most productive failures.

## The electron campaign completes (ELEC-023 through ELEC-043)

**The stationarity saga, and its resolution.** ELEC-023's minimality census discovered that
the campaign's residual metric had been VOID for ten claims -- decomposing a gradient over
constraints that were not binding -- and that the celebrated terminus was not a minimum. What
followed was five sessions of instrument work: the constraint-aware metric (ELEC-028), the
stale-preconditioner discovery that had understated it sixfold (ELEC-029), a run that in 75
seconds recovered more than five previous sessions combined, and finally a census at a
converging method's terminus (ELEC-030) that came within one direction of certifying -- then
ELEC-031 found that census had been SCALE-BLIND, and the corrections were filed.

**The verdicts that closed the campaign.** The tangent curvature is positive-definite on all
105 free directions (ELEC-033): the saddle worry, live since ELEC-023, is retired -- IF
stationarity is reached, the state is a genuine minimum. But the state is not stationary
smoothly, and ELEC-035's Clarke test (properly powered at 202 samples, after a first attempt
with 8 samples in 107 dimensions was caught as vacuous) shows it is not Clarke-stationary
either. Five unrelated search strategies all recover the same 1e-6 per round, which makes the
rate a property of the SEARCH PROBLEM. The landscape is benign; finding descent is the whole
difficulty.

**The structural obstacles, quantified.** ELEC-036 turned the form-factor problem from a
sentence into a trilemma: rms charge radius 21 fm against sub-1e-3 fm bounds, with branches A
and B carrying the SAME factor because the two anchors overdetermine one length. ELEC-037
proved a scale-invariant NO-GO -- the form-factor failure and the cross-sector clash scale as
s^-1 and s^+1, their product invariant to machine precision, so no length unit satisfies both.
The one-medium declaration was then taken deliberately (ELEC-038), priced (ELEC-039: the
counting convention is worth four orders), and its consequences followed: the electron is a
TWO-STRAND bound pair and a twentyfold local compression of the medium (ELEC-041), bound by
tension, field and contact -- three mechanisms the medium supplies -- with topological
protection IMPOSED rather than derived (ELEC-042).

**ELEC-043, the campaign's strongest negative.** The collective number was derived rather
than assumed, from the one thing the strand model fixes without freedom: transverse waves
travel at exactly c. Causality gives n_t ~ 1 where the framework needs 1e8. The route from a
classical medium to a quantum of action, carried since QGATE-008, does not survive its own
physics.

## The quantum sector opens (HBAR-001 through HBAR-010)

**A better route, and its limits.** A standing wave's action is S = pi T A^2/(2c): THE LENGTH
CANCELS, giving the universality hbar requires and reconnection never had, and a normal mode
is causally self-consistent by construction (HBAR-001). But the coupling that makes coherence
possible does not SELECT a scale -- the modes carrying hbar form a continuum (HBAR-002), and
four candidate quantizers were tested with three failing outright (HBAR-003). The fourth,
angular momentum, gives L = 2S exactly: SPIN AND ACTION QUANTIZATION ARE ONE GAP.

**Topology closed as a family.** HBAR-004: writhe is quadratic in amplitude like the action,
but it is DIMENSIONLESS, so a topological condition fixes a ratio and never a scale. No
topological route can supply a universal quantum of action.

**What hbar turns out to be here.** HBAR-005: no combination of the medium's constants gives
hbar, and L_hbar = 74.7 strand spacings -- hbar is MESOSCOPIC, the action of a patch ~4.3 fm
across, which means the framework predicts genuine sub-quantum structure below that scale and
supplies the scale the corpus's pilot-wave work never had. HBAR-006 turns that into a
prediction: non-Born statistics are a NUCLEAR-scale effect, invisible atomically.

**And what its constancy costs.** HBAR-007: the equation of state making hbar universal is
UNIQUE (an attractive inverse-square inter-strand potential), physically natural
(dipole-dipole), and UNSTABLE -- stability and constancy are purchased against each other at
a 1e12 fine-tuning. HBAR-008 makes the dipole concrete: it gives the required form exactly and
zero magnitude by symmetry, so net attraction demands an anisotropic vacuum. HBAR-009 shows
that anisotropy CANNOT be the compression matter produces (four independent failures, one of
them a contradiction with hbar's measured constancy in vacuum) -- leaving one convergence:
HBAR-007's cosmological escape and HBAR-008's preferred direction are ONE requirement.
HBAR-010 then prices it: a comoving medium is excluded by 7.5e4, so the strand lattice must be
rigid against expansion to one part in 2e5.

## The cross-sector family, and a prediction that held (XSEC-001 through XSEC-003)

**The collision.** XSEC-001: the gravity sector's best result (g_dagger = c H0/2pi, confirmed
on 155 SPARC galaxies at zero free parameters) contains an expansion rate; the quantum sector
had just forbidden the medium from expanding. Two programmes run side by side for a year
collided the first time they were made to answer to each other. Exactly one reading survives --
g_dagger = c^2/(2 pi L) with L the medium's FIXED extent -- and it converts a fit into a
falsifiable prediction: the acceleration scale must NOT evolve with redshift.

**The local test.** XSEC-002: an independently written pipeline on the shipped SPARC data
reproduces the zero-parameter prediction to 0.9 percent. An apparent correlation of g_dagger
with galaxy scale (p = 0.025) was traced to SAMPLING, not physics -- galaxies whose curves
never reach the low-acceleration regime cannot constrain where it begins -- and vanishes under
quality cuts.

**The test that mattered.** XSEC-003, on RC100 (Nestor Shachar et al. 2023): 100 discs at
z = 0.6-2.5. With the estimator's 13 percent bias measured on local data BEFORE the high-z
numbers were read, g_dagger comes out 1.878e-10, 1.384e-10 and 1.284e-10 in three redshift
bins against a constant prediction of 1.225e-10 and an H(z)-tracking prediction rising to
4.0e-10. Hypothesis B fails at 4.2 and 7.4 sigma; the trend is -0.381 +/- 0.392 against
+1.111 predicted. THE ACCELERATION SCALE DOES NOT TRACK H(z).

## What stands, said plainly

**There is still no physical electron.** No proven minimum, no spin, no charge quantization,
no g-factor, and a form factor four orders too large -- which no amount of optimization
touches. The mass calibration remains an identification by assumption.

**hbar is diagnosed, not derived.** The corpus now knows precisely what is missing -- a phase,
and a preferred direction that must come from cosmology rather than dynamics -- and has
supplied neither.

**The medium's parameters are inherited, not verified.** T = 1.70e3 N and rho = 5.67e18 kg/m^3
carry the whole HBAR chain, and if they are wrong every dimensional number moves.

**And the best result is a survival, not a discovery.** MOND also predicts a constant
acceleration scale; XSEC-003 discriminates among modified-gravity variants without uniquely
supporting ropes over them. The framework's distinctive claim is the INTERPRETATION of c/H0 as
a medium extent, and that interpretation does not yet buy an observable MOND lacks. Finding one
is the programme's deepest strategic need. What can be said is that the test's conclusion
depends on neither T nor rho -- only on constant-versus-tracking -- which makes it the most
robust thing in this release even though the chain that motivated it is not.

## POST-RELEASE CORRECTION (2026-07-31)

The headline result of this release does not stand as written. XSEC-004 and XSEC-005,
registered the day after the cut, establish that RC100's M_baryon is a FITTED parameter of
its rotation-curve model rather than an independent measurement -- in 22 percent of galaxies
it is smaller than the observed stellar mass, which no gas correction can produce. XSEC-003's
test therefore compared a quantity against a model that had already seen it, and its 7.4 sigma
exclusion of the H(z)-tracking hypothesis is void.

A photometric re-run (XSEC-005) gives non-monotonic, mutually inconsistent bins supporting
neither hypothesis, and an independent replication on KROSS (XSEC-004) cannot discriminate
because its assumption spread exceeds the signal fivefold.

WHAT STANDS: XSEC-001's cross-sector collision and the single surviving reading; XSEC-002's
LOCAL reproduction of the zero-parameter acceleration scale to 0.9 percent, which uses SPARC's
photometric and genuinely independent baryonic masses.

WHAT DOES NOT: any claim about the acceleration scale's behaviour with redshift. It is
UNMEASURED -- not confirmed, not refuted.
