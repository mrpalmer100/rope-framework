## Part I — The field-tensor dictionary closes

`EM-016`, the complete mapping from the rope's mechanical state to the
electromagnetic field tensor, had carried four explicit debts since it was
assembled. All four fell in one day, each to a theorem with its defeater
named **before** the attempt:

**(iv) Uniqueness — EM-017.** Any mechanical assignment predicting the same
force on every test winding at every velocity yields the same (E, B)
pointwise: E from a static probe under the registered operational
definition, B by explicit inversion of v × B on three independent
velocities (residual 3.8e-16 — constructed, not asserted, because the bar
named velocity-degeneracy as a way to fail), F_μν pointwise, potentials up
to gauge by Poincaré on EM-003's dF = 0. The duality rotation, pre-named as
the defeater, fails twice: arithmetically the static term detects it, and
structurally a Z-charge ontology with no magnetic monopole cannot represent
the rotated sources.

**(iii) The scalar potential's channel — EM-018.** Over the closed inventory
{transverse, longitudinal, screw}: the light carrier is excluded by a
computed Helmholtz argument (every transverse mode obeys k·e = 0, hence is
divergence-free; a gradient field is curl-free; the subspaces meet only at
zero). The screw mode is excluded structurally — it carries winding, so it
*is* the charge, not its mediator. The longitudinal channel is the only
survivor, and its gaplessness — derived in EM-RECON-012 for the entirely
unrelated reason that u is gauge so no mass term is writable — is exactly
what long-range Coulomb requires, a gapped mediator giving Yukawa.

**(ii) The inertial term — EM-019.** The easy Newtonian move was ruled out
in advance: FND-REL-002 says there is no material velocity. What works
instead is that *the same shift symmetry which forbids a mass term permits
the inertial one*, because a gauge label's rate of change is observable even
when the label is not. Computed: under a → a + c₀ the mass term changes by
c₀(c₀+2a)/2 and the mixed term by c₀ ∂a/∂t, while (∂a/∂t)² and (∇a)² are
exactly invariant.

**(i) Σ's absolute value — EM-021.** The one that looked hardest and turned
on reading a source. Σ is not a free constant: it is its own definition
T₀·n_L evaluated on the registered mesh (n_L = 1/a² per family), giving
6.0e36–9.0e37 J/m³. The ~1e25 that made it look independent was never a
measurement but a **lower bound saturated by an assumed equality** —
EM-RECON-014's own file says "equality = the identification" — and QGATE-007
withdrew the saturating assumption. A value eleven orders above a lower
bound satisfies it.

**Grade raised to Derived (EM-022, the author's act), with three
conditionalities carried on its face and a falsifier armed:** Σ inherits the
κ_pack floor spread held by a Conjecture-grade form; the inertial
coefficient is matched to EM-RECON-025 rather than independently derived;
its uniqueness assumes locality. **If the floors move materially or FND-037's
form is refuted, the grade returns to adjudication rather than keeping the
label by seniority.** The grade change is registered as its *own* auditable
act rather than folded into the discharges, because three of the four came
from the same session, and a grade raised by its own evidence-producing
session cannot otherwise be checked.

## Part II — The photon sector, prosecuted and disclosed

Ultra-high-energy photons (LHAASO's Galactic PeV gamma rays, arriving from
across the sky) **cannot be carried by the medium as currently specified**. Four escapes were
prosecuted and all four closed, which is what makes the problem sharp rather
than vague -- the failure is of the registered length inventory, not a proof
that no rope medium could do it:

- the loaded continuum (`FND-058`) — removes the lattice, not the strands;
- the collective mode (`FND-059`) — anisotropic, contradicting the corpus's
  *own* Derived isotropy theorem;
- any operator-shaped fix (`FND-060`) — closed as a **class**: ω²(k) is
  periodic for arbitrary coupling range and Gershgorin bounds the disordered
  case, so E_max ~ ħc/a always. **The ceiling is discreteness, not the
  nearest-neighbour approximation.**
- tuning a length — blocked: both registered lengths are electron-anchored.

**An external reviewer then corrected us (`FND-061`), and the correction
stands.** FND-060 bounded the coarse lattice displacement field, but
EM-RECON-025's registered *light* branch is ω² = (T₀/μ)q² — continuum in q,
no Brillouin cutoff; crossings couple rather than sample. The implication
that the theorem bounded *light* is withdrawn. The escape closes anyway on
the isotropy result, but the demand is restated from "supply a length below
1.41e-22 m" to **"supply isotropy at high k"** — a strictly wider target.

**Route (c) adopted (`FND-062`, the author's act):** the limitation is
registered openly in `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`, first section, with its number,
diagnosis, four closed escapes and precise scope.

**Scope, stated precisely:** the transverse-wave mechanics are **not**
refuted. The collective mode exists, propagates, carries exactly two
polarizations, and supports the derived couplings. Every result at
accessible energies stands untouched.

## Corrections made and kept

This release contains three self-corrections, all registered rather than
quietly patched:

1. **`EM-020`'s DIFFERENT-OBJECTS verdict was wrong** and was corrected
   within the same session. It compared a *bound* to a *value* across 10.6
   orders and inferred distinct quantities. **Lesson registered: a numerical
   gap is evidence of distinct objects only if both numbers are of the same
   epistemic kind.**
2. **A stale-value error**, caught by the author: an in-session estimate
   priced Σ from EM-016's blocker text without checking the FND-030 series
   that had already pinned it. **Lesson registered: a claim's blocker text
   ages independently of the claims that would discharge it.**
3. **A process failure, accepted on the record:** the photon contradiction
   sat registered with its escape unadjudicated across two claims while work
   continued on top of it, and FND-MATTER-049's reopening tripwire fired at
   10¹¹× without halting anything. **New house rule: a registered
   contradiction whose escape is unrun is a blocking item, not a footnote.**

A downstream correction also belongs here: `EM-RECON-015`'s vacuum mass
density (~1e8 kg/m³) was computed from the superseded Σ and is stale by
10–12 orders. The live figures are 4.0e18 kg/m³ on Σ_eff (matching FND-031's
independently registered fence) and 2.0e20 on 3T₀/a². **The vacuum-energy
problem that claim flagged is larger than it states, not smaller.**
